A door leaf is a leaf mesh with the hinge translation on its node, so the hinge got baked into the geometry and the leaf rotated about the tile origin. It had nothing to do with the merge (the merged GLB had the correct node translations; gltfpack was not used). Fix at the source: `worldpack_mesh.write_glb` gives every `isDoor` node one empty child node `<door>_hinge` (a mesh WITH a child is skipped by applyTransforms); `read_glb` skips mesh-less nodes. Works for split, merged and layer tiles, no change to the layer/merge flow, doors stay in the cheap merged buildings tile (65 doors). Live: `_doors['bld_house_041_door1']` keeps position (-25.64, 5.78, -44.22) and the hinge world position stays fixed while `ds=1` rotates it; leaf centre moves 0.05 scene units (a 3.5 ft leaf), not 230 ft. Rotation: +1.5708 rad about +y (doorAnimateAngle), the leaf swings into the room (seen from above: leaf ends up along the right-hand jamb inside the opening); interior door `bld_house_041_idoor_f0_1` behaves the same; `ds=0` closes both back to the original centre (3.294, 0.67, 2.389) and rotation 0. Flags reset to ds 0 afterwards. Tests: `test_door_hinge.py` (door node keeps translation and has a child, merge round trip, simulated applyTransforms bakes nothing); `test_door_compress`, `test_interiors.MeshTests`, `test_worldpack_detail` still pass.

ROOF RIDGE. `shingle.png` (and slate, red_tile) are NOT broken at the wrap: the wrap step (74 -> 111 luminance) equals every course edge inside the tile (largest inner step 54 vs wrap 42), thatch/timber are smooth; so no texture was changed. The cause is the UV phase: roof UV v was world-absolute plus a random per-house offset, so the ridge ended at an arbitrary row of the course (often the bright top row). `world_uv(anchor_v=...)` + `ridge_phase(mat)` now put the highest vertex of every sloped roof face on the darkest row of the average course of the roof texture (found by FFT/fold of the row profile). Test: `RoofSeam` (wrap no harder than the largest inner step in both axes for all roof textures; ridge on a dark row; anchor exact). Live (final3_roof_ridge.jpg): ridge reads as a dark course edge, no light line. Re-exported with `--roof-mode per_scene`, roofs.glb re-pushed.

EAST APRON STREAKS (cause found, fixed). Not apron, not slope, not the albedo edge columns (numerically normal). It is the TERRAIN mesh: its height grid is 126 x 148 nodes = 312.5 ft wide but the frame is 306.5 ft, so `build_terrain` UVs run to 1.0196 (u) and 1.0158 (v); the terrain texture uses the CLAMP sampler (wrap 33071), so the last texel row/column was smeared over the 6 ft strip along the east and south edges (stripes constant across the strip). Hiding the apron kept the band; the band was there on the terrain tile. Fix: `build_terrain` mirrors UVs back into the image (`1 - |1 - uv|`), test `TerrainUV`. Live after the fix: the stripes are gone (a mirrored strip of normal grass texture remains, final3_east_edge_after.jpg). Cost: terrain geometry bytes unchanged, UVs only.
