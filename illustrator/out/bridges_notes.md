# Bridges (bridge_maker.py)

## Diagnosis: the floating road
`region_extract.py` clipped child-map streets to the work area but copied the parent's per-vertex `elevations_ft` unclipped. `worldpack.export_world` then did `zip(points, elevations)`, so a clipped street got the elevations of the first vertices of the PARENT line. Dyers Walk child: Warden Street (str_014) 6 points / 43 elevations -> deck z 657 over terrain 643..647 (11-14 ft too high); `road_conformed_heights` then pulled the terrain up to it (embankment), and the lane str_020 (z 642) ran beneath. Same bug on str_008/019/020 and, in the commoners ring, str_pilgrim_road_1, str_022, str_024.
Fix at source: `region_extract.clip_vertex_values` (arc-length resample for the clipped vertices). Existing data repaired with `bridge_maker.py fix-elevations --place P` (done for the three Dyers Walk / Commoners Ring places; reads the parent's streets via region.json). After the fix Dyers Walk has no crossing that needs a bridge (detect returns none at the 4 ft / 3 ft thresholds); before/after: `out/bridges/dyers_before.png`, `dyers_after.png`.

## Crossing report (`detect`)
Per crossing: `id` (`brg_<road>_<n>`), `type` gully | water | road | existing, `road`, `s0_ft/s1_ft/span_ft` (arc length on the road), `deck_above_ground_ft`, `others` (water id + wet interval + surface, or road id + angle + level), `clearance_needed`. Floating = deck > 4 ft over carved terrain; road crossings = centrelines intersect (not near an end) with > 3 ft level difference (upper road gets the bridge); water = road inside a water polygon (+3 ft banks, +5 ft abutment margin); `existing` = the pack's deck-only bridge roads (Bridgehollow's Pilgrim Bridge).

## Clearance and terrain
Envelope per crossing (u along the span): water = surface .. surface + freeboard (4 ft, reduced to what the road level allows, min 0.5 ft, reported as `freeboard_ft` vs `wanted_ft`) across the wet width + 3 ft banks; road/path below = 12 ft x 8 ft default projected by the crossing angle (`clearance_w_ft/h_ft` override); gully = the low middle with 8 ft head room. Piers/abutments/bents are only placed outside envelopes (arch piers are nudged to the envelope edge or dropped); arches are lifted (springing) and flattened (rise 0.5 -> 0.14) until the intrados clears every envelope and the ground profile, and still fits under the deck; if nothing fits the plan is `ok: false` with a `deficit_m`, and `apply` tries the other presets and keeps the one that misses least (canon-material mismatches are warned in provenance). Timber stringers / slab thickness are checked the same way. `check_clearance` samples every vertex, edge midpoint and centroid against the envelopes (tests: 0 hits from 10 to 200 ft spans, all presets).
Terrain: the mesh is not cut. `bridge_maker.conformed_heights` (hooked into `worldpack_mesh.build_parts`) leaves the span out of the road-bed conform so the gully / river bed keeps its shape; the road ribbon is removed over the span (`gaps` on the road entity, `trim_runs`); the ground profile under the deck (max over the deck width +/- 2 ft) is stored in the entity and the opening is built above it. End blocks follow the rising ground. Deck ends meet the ribbons (road z + 0.25 m lift); arch decks get a parabolic hump (max 3 % of the span, end slope < 13 %).

## Data
`world/<place>/bridges.json` (generated, status proposed, provenance, `removed` list, `locked` = hand-tuned via Apply to selected; canon streets untouched). `inject` writes `export/entities/bridges.json` (type `bridges`, schema enum extended), `gaps` on the roads, manifest entry + hashes. `build_parts` makes one Part per bridge (layer `bridges`, split granularity `entity`, ids `brg_<road>_<n>` stable, pivot/placement from the normal split). 2D map: `render_world.py` draws outline, inner parapet line, pier and arch hatch / plank hatch from `bridge_maker.symbols_for_place` on the Roads layer (`<id>_outline`, ... ), appears on the next Render.
Workflow: `bridge_maker.py apply --place P` (re-exports the pack, detects, writes bridges.json, injects) -> `worldpack_split.py split` -> `merge` -> `foundry_push.py push-models` -> `skybox.py set-flag --scene-uuid .. --preset clear_day --apply` (push-models resets the skybox flag; `--yaw` belongs to `skybox.py generate`, not set-flag).

## Presets (`presets/bridges/*.json`, materials via existing pbr/building-style ids)
stone_arch (rubble wall `mat_wall_stone`, `mat_kerb` voussoirs/springers/coping, cutwaters, cobble deck), brick_arch (`mat_s_brick_*`, stone springers + coping), flat_cut_stone (slabs on coursed piers, low coped parapet), wooden_truss (log stringers, plank deck, bents, king-post or X-braced log truss sides, seeded rough variation), plank_footbridge (stringers, planks, post-and-rail). Panel parameters map to preset params: width_ft, clearance_w_ft/h_ft, parapet_h_ft, n_arches, rise_ratio, seed. Triangle counts 90-3800 for 60 ft spans. No LOD.

## v2: new styles, terrain clearance, supports below ground
* New presets (families `log`, `covered`, `rope`; all in the panel Bridges tab and in `out/bridges/sheet_v2.png`): `rough_log` (4-5 raw logs side by side, seeded diameters / lengths / splintered ends, bearing on bank rocks, max span 26 ft), `covered_timber` (plank deck on pile bents = splayed A-frame piles with X bracing, or `support: piers` = stone piers with cutwaters; stone abutments; gable roof `roof: shingle|tile` that overhangs the portals, posts, knee braces inside and under the eaves, plank side walls 3.9 ft high, optional `flower_boxes`; post spacing, bays and roof follow the span), `rope_plank` (catenary sag, foot + hand ropes, hanging verticals, anchor posts at both ends, seeded missing / broken / hanging planks, uneven plank widths and gaps; `decay` 0..0.5, `sag_ratio`). Panel fields: supports, roof, flower boxes, sag, decay.
* Rope sag clamp (`_plan_rope`): sag = min(wanted, allowed) where allowed keeps the underside (plank + a hanging plank) >= `min_clear_ft` (2.5) above the highest ground and on top of the water / road envelopes along the span; the comfort margin fades in away from the banks (the ground rises to the deck there), the hard limit (ground + 0.1 m) applies everywhere. A clamp is reported in `plan.notes`; if even zero sag cannot clear, `ok: false`.
* Deck height fix (all styles): `make_entity` samples the highest terrain under the deck footprint (9 samples across, +/- 2 ft beyond the edges, every 2 ft) and computes `context.deck_lift` (`compute_lift`): deck must be >= 0.5 ft above that terrain everywhere, slope-limited to 12 %, capped at 6 ft (a warning is added to provenance when terrain still rises above it: trim the terrain). `make_plan` adds the lift to the deck line; the builders add an approach ramp (`_finish`, 10 % slope, from the road level up to the lifted deck end, no bottom face). Ends with no terrain problem are unchanged (`deck_lift` empty, no ramp). Old entities without `deck_lift` keep their old shape until re-applied.
* Supports: every foot goes >= 1.5 ft (`found_ft`, min 1.5) below the lowest ground under its footprint (window +/- 1.2 m for bents/piers/posts, ground profile = min over the deck width +/- 2 ft of the final terrain). `plan.feet` lists every foot (`kind, u, z`), checked by tests against bumpy synthetic ground and bilinear samples of a real raster.
* Tests: 32 in `test_bridge_maker.py` (new: NewStyleTests, TerrainClearanceTests, DeckEndsAfterLiftTests).
* Limits: the terrain mesh is not cut (lift + ramp only); feet are checked against the sampled raster, not the triangulated terrain mesh (the 1.5 ft margin covers the difference); the rope bridge posts sit 3 ft behind the deck ends and use the ground at the end of the span; the new styles are not chosen automatically by `choose_preset` (pick them in the panel or `--preset`); the white-ish rock material of `rough_log` is `mat_wall_stone`.

## v3: fixes from the Foundry live test (2026-11-08)
* rope_plank: wanted sag = `sag_ratio` (0.10, i.e. 8-12 % of the span, was 7.5 %). `_plan_rope` finds the smallest RAISE of the whole rope line (anchors) that lets the deck hang that far and still clear ground / water / road envelopes (bisection, capped by `raise_max_ft` 8); the deck ends then sit above the road, `lift_ends` grows and `_finish` builds a steeper approach ramp (`rope_ramp_slope` 0.22). Anchor posts are taller (rail + 1 m above the deck end) with raking back-legs; the sag is clamped only if even the max raise is not enough (reported in `plan.notes`, `plan.rope.clamped`). The deck sways sideways (zero at the anchors), at least 4 planks are always missing / broken (decay default 0.22), hanging planks drop at most 0.35 m (what the plan reserves). In the rebuilt scratch the rope lane needed no raise (deck far above the water): sag 6.4 ft on 64 ft, not clamped. The raise path is covered by tests, not by a live shot.
* rough_log: no log is longer than `max_log_ft` (26 ft). Crossings that need more get log TRESTLES (2 splayed legs per side, cap log, chocks under each log, lashings, diagonal brace; feet go >= 1.5 ft below the bed) and every lane is several logs laid end to end with the joints on the caps; up to `max_span_ft` 70 ft (2-3 bays up to ~66 ft). Trestles may stand in water envelopes (`plan.wade`), never in a road / path envelope. The old 28 ft case is ok now, notes say "3 bays on 2 log trestles, longest log 24.6 ft".
* covered_timber: bents / piers are no longer pushed out of the water envelope: they stand at even spacing (max bay 22 ft) from below the bed up to the beams, bent = slim splayed timber A-frames with X bracing, pier = stone block with cutwaters; `check_clearance` exempts the support windows (`plan.wade`) for WATER envelopes only (beams between supports must still clear; a road below still keeps supports out). The scratch pair (piles + shingle, piers + tile) differs clearly in the live scene.
* `final_heights` is now the carved terrain (road grading on = no conform), i.e. what `build_parts` actually builds; `graded=False` keeps the old conformed terrain.
* `bridge_maker.audit_mesh` / CLI `audit --place P`: feet and deck vs the terrain TRIANGLES (`road_grade.mesh_height`). Scratch result: every foot >= 1.4 ft below the mesh, deck >= 0.8 ft above the highest terrain across its width everywhere and >= 0.8 ft at both ends. (A +-1 ft window beyond the foot on the 40 deg channel bank reports < 1.5 ft; the foot's own footprint, +-0.3 ft, is what is audited.)
* Scratch place: `tools/make_bridge_scratch.py` regenerates heightmap, water.json (centerline + widths_ft) and the lane humps; lanes: rough_log 40 ft water, flat 12, stone_arch 36, brick_arch 36 (same crossing), covered piles 52, covered piers 52 (same crossing), rope 44, truss 48. Not committed (world/zz_bridge_scratch is untracked).
* Tests: 41 in `test_bridge_maker.py` (new: trestles, rope sag / raise / sway / missing planks, covered supports in water + variants differ + road-below, mesh audit all styles, audit catches a floating foot).
* Foundry: pushed to ZZ Bridge Test (Scene.umSwyrt2sZYMAWvF) with split / merge none (layer_v003) / push-models / push-scene-3d / padding push / skybox set-flag; screenshots `foundry_screens/bridge_v3_<style>_ground.jpg` (camera in the channel) and `_bank.jpg` (bank level, under / beside the deck).
