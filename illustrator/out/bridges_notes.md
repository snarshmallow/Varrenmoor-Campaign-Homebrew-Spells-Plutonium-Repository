# Bridges (bridge_maker.py)

## Diagnosis: the floating road
`region_extract.py` clipped child-map streets to the work area but copied the parent's per-vertex `elevations_ft` unclipped. `worldpack.export_world` then did `zip(points, elevations)`, so a clipped street got the elevations of the first vertices of the PARENT line. Dyers Walk child: Warden Street (str_014) 6 points / 43 elevations -> deck z 657 over terrain 643..647 (11-14 ft too high); `road_conformed_heights` then pulled the terrain up to it (embankment), and the lane str_020 (z 642) ran beneath. Same bug on str_008/019/020 and, in the commoners ring, str_pilgrim_road_1, str_022, str_024.
Fix at source: `region_extract.clip_vertex_values` (arc-length resample for the clipped vertices). Existing data repaired with `bridge_maker.py fix-elevations --place P` (done for the three Dyers Walk / Commoners Ring places; reads the parent's streets via region.json). After the fix Dyers Walk has no crossing that needs a bridge (detect returns none at the 4 ft / 3 ft thresholds); before/after: `out/bridges/dyers_before.png`, `dyers_after.png`.

## Crossing report (`detect`)
Per crossing: `id` (`brg_<road>_<n>`), `type` gully | water | road | existing, `road`, `s0_ft/s1_ft/span_ft` (arc length on the road), `deck_above_ground_ft`, `others` (water id + wet interval + surface, or road id + angle + level), `clearance_needed`. Floating = deck > 4 ft over carved terrain; road crossings = centrelines intersect (not near an end) with > 3 ft level difference (upper road gets the bridge); water = road inside a water polygon (+3 ft banks, +5 ft abutment margin); `existing` = the pack's deck-only bridge roads (Bridgehollow's Pilgrim Bridge).

## Clearance and terrain
Envelope per crossing (u along the span): water = surface .. surface + freeboard (4 ft, reduced to what the road level allows, min 0.5 ft, reported as `freeboard_ft` vs `wanted_ft`) across the wet width + 3 ft banks; road/path below = 12 ft x 8 ft default projected by the crossing angle (`clearance_w_ft/h_ft` override); gully = the low middle with 8 ft head room. Piers/abutments/bents are only placed outside envelopes (arch piers are nudged to the envelope edge or dropped); arches are lifted (springing) and flattened (rise 0.5 -> 0.14) until the intrados clears every envelope and the ground profile, and still fits under the deck; if nothing fits the plan is `ok: false` with a `deficit_m`, and `apply` tries the other presets and keeps the one that misses least (canon-material mismatches are warned in provenance). Timber stringers / slab thickness are checked the same way. `check_clearance` samples every vertex, edge midpoint and centroid against the envelopes (tests: 0 hits from 10 to 200 ft spans, all presets).
Terrain: the mesh is not cut. `bridge_maker.conformed_heights` (hooked into `worldpack_mesh.build_parts`) leaves the span out of the road-bed conform so the gully / river bed keeps its shape; the road ribbon is removed over the span (`gaps` on the road entity, `trim_runs`); the ground profile under the deck (max over the deck width +/- 2 ft) is stored in the entity and the opening is built above it. End blocks follow the rising ground. Deck ends meet the ribbons (road z + 0.25 m lift); arch decks get a parabolic hump (max 3 % of the span, end slope < 13 %).

## Data
`world/<place>/bridges.json` (generated, status proposed, provenance, `removed` list, `locked` = hand-tuned via Apply to selected; canon streets untouched). `inject` writes `export/entities/bridges.json` (type `bridges`, schema enum extended), `gaps` on the roads, manifest entry + hashes. `build_parts` makes one Part per bridge (layer `bridges`, split granularity `entity`, ids `brg_<road>_<n>` stable, pivot/placement from the normal split). 2D map: `render_world.py` draws outline, inner parapet line, pier and arch hatch / plank hatch from `bridge_maker.symbols_for_place` on the Roads layer (`<id>_outline`, ... ), appears on the next Render.
Workflow: `bridge_maker.py apply --place P` (re-exports the pack, detects, writes bridges.json, injects) -> `worldpack_split.py split` -> `merge` -> `foundry_push.py push-models` -> `skybox.py set-flag --scene-uuid .. --preset clear_day --apply` (push-models resets the skybox flag; `--yaw` belongs to `skybox.py generate`, not set-flag).

## Presets (`presets/bridges/*.json`, materials via existing pbr/building-style ids)
stone_arch (rubble wall `mat_wall_stone`, `mat_kerb` voussoirs/springers/coping, cutwaters, cobble deck), brick_arch (`mat_s_brick_*`, stone springers + coping), flat_cut_stone (slabs on coursed piers, low coped parapet), wooden_truss (log stringers, plank deck, bents, king-post or X-braced log truss sides, seeded rough variation), plank_footbridge (stringers, planks, post-and-rail). Panel parameters map to preset params: width_ft, clearance_w_ft/h_ft, parapet_h_ft, n_arches, rise_ratio, seed. Triangle counts 90-3800 for 60 ft spans. No LOD.
