          Collision, sight and walls (3D Canvas)
  
          Collision: the tile blocks player movement through it and tokens stand on it (terrain, roads, floors); costs no render time. Applies to Push models and, with Set collision, to the scene's existing tiles.
          
  
          
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
  
          
  
          
    
    
  
          
  
          Sight (tile flag): the tile blocks token vision, fog exploration, light sources and sound radii, no wall documents needed (3D Canvas builds the polygons from the meshes; an open model door lets vision through). Costs worker time, not render time: buildings about 3 ms per light/vision polygon off the render thread, foliage about 1 ms; a door toggle re-sends its whole tile (20 ms on a merged 25-house tile, so merge buildings with the "doors" preset). Applies to Push models and, with Set collision, to existing tiles.

          Max gradedeg
          
  
          Max cross slopedeg
          
  
  
  
  
          To change ONE road (class, width, surface, kerb, grading) use the Roads tab.
          
  
          Roads sit just above the real terrain mesh so the ground never covers them. Smoothing blends the raw ground height with a smoothed profile along the road; roads meet bridges and each other at the same height. Cut/fill is off by default and changes the terrain mesh; warnings and cut/fill amounts are written to placements.json (road_grade).             Selected bridge
            
  
  
            
  
            
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
  
            
  
            
  
  
            
  
            The deck keeps the road level at both ends unless terrain rises above it there: then the deck is lifted just clear (max 6 ft) and a short approach ramp joins it to the road. Piers, piles, posts and rocks always end at least 1.5 ft below the lowest ground under them. Openings are sized from the clearance below (water surface + banks + freeboard, or the road/path passing under, default 8 ft x 12 ft); piers and abutments stay outside them, the road ribbon is removed over the span and the terrain under it is left as it is. The map symbol (outline, parapets, piers) appears on the next Render. Needs a Pack export and Split (Foundry tab) to reach the 3D scene. Notes: illustrator/out/bridges_notes.md.
