    Label style

    Wrapping (areas, buildings, rooms)

    Abbreviations come from presets/label_abbreviations.json (edit it: Street = St., Authority = Auth., ...). Lines, widths and ratio are per category like the rest of the style.

    Halo / backing / knock-out

    Category rows only store what you change; "All" is the base. Knock-out cuts real geometry (contours and, per target below, linework) so it stays editable. Textured raster fills (ART - Fill ...) are not cut: use halo or backing box there.

    Leaders (offset labels)

    Move labels by hand (callouts)

    Every offset label is a callout: text <id>_label, leader <id>_leader (+ _leader_casing) and anchor dot <id>_leader_dot. Drag the text with the Selection tool (not the leader); the leader keeps its anchor and re-routes. Drag the dot (Leader Anchors layer) to move the anchor.

      Town area (irregular lasso fitted to the terrain)

      The outline is drawn on layers TOWN AREA (editable closed path) and TOWN AREA RINGS (authority/commoner guides). Edit it with the Direct Selection tool, press Capture lasso, then Generate. A lasso you draw yourself is used as drawn.

      Rings (radius, street width, lot area)

      no candidate

      Generate writes a candidate model and draws it; the saved world model stays as it was until you Accept.

      Map fills (textured, by feature type)

      Streets, plazas, buildings, fields and water get a texture chosen by their type (road, street, lane, alley, bridge; house, civic, inn, mill, barn...; crop; river, lake) from the style's types map. Rasters go on ART - Fill Fields / Water / Streets / Buildings below LINES and are replaced on each run; the linework and labels stay vector on top.

      Fills per part (flat colours, legacy)

      Flat fills are named swatches ("VB Streets - ...") set on the named layers. Scripts cannot create graphic styles: keep them in the .ait template (see illustrator/README.md).

      Zoom-in child map of a district

      Parent = the place chosen at the top. The child keeps the parent's feet and entity ids; only the view scale changes. Details are generated (not canon).
