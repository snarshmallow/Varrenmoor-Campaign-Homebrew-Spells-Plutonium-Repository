Has a small non-combat animal companion (the player chose not to make it mechanical; "he's just there"). The player made a character model and was asked to email it to the GM for a 3D model.

## Europa's background (player's important details)
- Raised by her **human father Agenor** ("Aggie" to the locals), who ran a successful, well-known but small **inn in Bridgehollow**. Europa was left with him by her traveller **drow (Eirwyn) mother, Telephassa**.
- Ran the inn alongside him, planning to take it over one day.
- Taught by her community to hate magic, but by her father only to fear and respect it. Small bits of magic slipped out now and then, especially when calming the lively chaos of the tavern. She never spoke of it.
- Agenor was badly injured in the wilderness beyond Bridgehollow on a foraging trip and **lost his staff, a keepsake from Telephassa**.
- As the wound became infected and untreatable, Europa ran the business, including foraging for ingredients. While out she found her father's staff, tried to retrieve it, and nearly fell into the same **ravine** that wounded him.
- **Saved at the last moment by her brother**, an **Eirwitch practitioner**, who was on his way to claim her and look for their mother. She refused to go with him, scared of his magic. He told her to **meet him at the Ossuary Exchange when the time was right**, where there would be more information on where their mother might be.
- She returned home with the staff to find her father dying. He urged her to leave and be everything she could be.
- She spent a week more in the city gathering gear and privately experimenting with her magic, buried her father, and took to the road, leaving the inn with old patrons and family friends. (What happens to the inn afterwards is unknown on purpose.)

## The GM's hook text for Europa (kept close to the original)
1. A half drow from Bridgehollow who has lived her whole life assuming her little magic was passed down from her unknown mother and easy to hide. When her father, who raised her, grew ill and she carried his work, she learned she had a **brother**, a practitioner of **Eirwitch magic**, when he saved her on an outing beyond the city. He said that when the "right time came" she could find him at the **Ossuary Exchange** and they could learn what happened to their mother together. For years she put it out of her head, until her father died, while her innate magic grew harder to contain. She decided to leave her old life, embrace the magic, and piece her family history together.
2. **The Ossuary Exchange:** a magical place on a different thread of the **Wyrd (plane)** from where she comes. It is an exchange of **bones and bodies**: traded, sold, leased, reclaimed, with every transfer of ownership recorded. Many people inhabit bodies that are not their original, some skeletal and some involuntarily; souls persist in bodies past decay. Everything runs under a **bureaucratic three-level, independent verification method** that gives a semblance of certainty the realm exists, and which supposedly prevents illegal and involuntary trade of bones ("or so you're told"). She has **no idea if bones or bodies can leave the exchange.** New magical wonders are frequently visible there, some terrifying.
3. **Bridgehollow's authorities:** shortly before her father's death something stirred among the local authorities. The **Agisters** (tax collectors / local authorities) delayed tax collection until later in the day and began patrols of several teams of **Haywards** (land guards) along the outer fields from dawn to dusk. The **Reave** (chief officer over the Agisters and the city finances) was visited early by the **Regarder** (administrative authority above him); neither has been seen since. The Regarder's **sleek black caravan and warhorses, with Iron Bailiffs** on guard, are stationed there.
4. **Fen-Reivers:** locals who work near the swamps whisper that **Fen-Reivers** have returned. Nobody she knows has seen one. They are the **Niorghast's** special assassins and spies. (**Niorghast** = the Lord of all, leader in succession and spirit to **Nior**, the First Lord; literally "Nior's Ghost". A **Verderant** is one step below the Niorghast in authority.) Livestock and small children disappear, with long clawed paths and bloody entrails left behind. They last appeared before she was born, and people say a Verderant came to town then. The disturbance mostly affects the inner workings of the town's authorities; day-to-day life is not harder, but the added security forced her to be very stealthy to slip past.
5. **The clue (a hand-drawn map):** her attempts to learn about the Ossuary Exchange fail, but she finds an old, over-folded hand-sketched parchment map tucked into the back cover of a book in her father's study. It shows a long path leaving Bridgehollow in two directions: **South**, leading south; and **South East**, curving around a mountain pass and then leading **north** (the Niorghast's fortress is to the north). The path that eventually leads north is marked by strange, chiselled, bold, unfamiliar symbols with the name **CLADIS**. The other direction is marked with an intricate illustration of **a Large Tree and an elderly man hanging from it.** Local research only tells her **Cladis is related to the Eirwitches.** On that lead she sets out to find help getting to the Ossuary Exchange.
