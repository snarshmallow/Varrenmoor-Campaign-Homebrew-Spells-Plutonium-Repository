# PART 7: SESSION 14, "THE THAW"
*A Lower Ossuary session for a level-4 party (plus Europa, if she joins). One session, about 3 to 4 hours. Written to sit after Part 6 of the GM Reference and use the same tags.*

**Tags (same as the rest of the guide)**
- **[COMMON]** anyone local says it freely. **[ASK]** shared if asked politely or if it's relevant. **[EARN]** only after a favor, a payment, a good Insight/Persuasion roll, or trust. **[SECRET]** DM only.
- **NEW** marks anything this section adds to canon. **SETTLED** marks GM rulings made for this session. **[UNSETTLED]** marks something you still need to decide. **[SUGGESTED]** is a default you can swap.
- New NPCs are specified in full in `Session_14_New_NPCs_Spec.md`. Scenes are in `Session_14_Scenes_Spec.md`.

**Standing rules carried over:** Werd is not Wyrd. Mwooreth speech is ordinary in-world. Nobody states the four-PC convergence or the cause of the Great Tangle. The sword and the egg never leave party custody without the players choosing it. Trainers give no extra charges.

**New proper nouns in this section: two.** **The Thaw** (what the secret group calls itself) and **Lakkar, Lady of Eternal Hunger** (the Rhime Goddess's name). Everything else is a person's name, an existing place, or a plain description ("the cold door", "the line", "the vessel", "the trail").

## 7.0 OVERVIEW (THE ONE-PAGE VERSION)

**The goal of the session.** The party finds out that Quill belongs to a small, careful group inside the Exchange, the Thaw, that is following a trail **Cladis** left behind when she was banished outside the Wyrd. The Thaw wants to bring her back so she can enlist the Rhime Goddess against the Niorghast. The party helps build the Ossuary end of a line to re-tie Cladis's thread, using their crafts. At the end of the night they learn the far end can only be tied **outside**, at the Eirwitch camp above **Bridgehollow**, where **Venfyura** keeps the fire, and they leave through an old door that opens onto that mountain road.

**What the party learns**
1. **SETTLED** Cladis, the ancient Eirwitch Winter Queen, was **banished outside the Wyrd**. Before she went, she left a **trail** so others could follow in her steps and help stop Nior.
2. **SETTLED** She reached the Rhime Goddess's Ice Palace by **breaking her own Wyrd thread, shredding it, and re-tying it outside the Wyrd**. She is alive out there, on a thread whose end is tied in the wrong place.
3. **NEW** One mark of her trail is an **open claim** in the Exchange's custody ledger, copied forward for about three hundred years: *"Held: the place where even time cannot leave. Tag: outstanding."* The same words are cut into the Stolen Shard.
4. **SETTLED** Quill's real aim: bring Cladis back so she can **enlist the Rhime Goddess**. The Goddess **needs a name** to act inside the Wyrd: **Lakkar, Lady of Eternal Hunger**. With it she can help fight the **Niorghast** and stop him **resurrecting Nior**.
5. **SETTLED** The Rhime Goddess **has noticed**. The palace is hers, and her shard is in the party's hands.
6. **The method** is a plain repair job: a **line** (a new length of thread), **knots** at both ends (anchors), a **bell** that tells Cladis where the new knot is, a **vessel** in case her body doesn't make the crossing, and **three agreeing records** so the Exchange's threshold accepts her and nothing else.
7. The far end must be tied at the gorge beside the Eirwitch camp, with the camp bell ringing. Venfyura keeps that camp. The bell's clapper has been seized in Bridgehollow.

**What it costs**
- Most of the day's craft charges (see 7.4; about 2 per crafter).
- Risk: Inspector Tallis of the Exchange is close to noticing. If he files his notice, the cold door is sealed for good and the party walks the long way.
- The Stolen Shard is needed (shown, measured, and later perhaps used as the line's sinker). It stays in party custody, but it is the Goddess's shard, and she is watching it.
- The Goddess's attention. Something very old and very cold now knows the party's names. Not a fight: a price, later (7.6b).
- A promise of silence. Breaking it in a common hall gets the cell's members fired or worse.

**How it ends.** At "the usual time" the cold door opens for one minute. A capsule comes through from the mountain side: *"Bell silent. Clapper taken to the Customs House. Bailiffs on the road. V. keeps the fire."* Frost writes a name on the Stolen Shard's blade. The Thaw can finish the Ossuary end. Only the party can tie the far end. The door will not open again if Tallis files tomorrow. They go now.

- **Why a name matters (say it in three sentences):** *Something outside the Wyrd can't touch anything inside it unless the Wyrd has a name for it, the same way the Exchange won't move a bone that isn't in the ledger. A name is the handle, like an entry key. Lakkar has been outside so long that her handle is gone, so someone whose thread is inside has to carry her name back in and say it.* That is why Cladis matters: her thread, once re-tied inside, can carry the name, and she knows how to bargain with Lakkar.
- **[SECRET]** What Lakkar wants in return is not settled (7.6b gives options). The party is now **entrusted with the name**: they know it, and it is theirs to carry or keep. Nobody in the Thaw says it aloud in a common room.

Return means **re-tying her thread inside the Wyrd**: a new length of line knotted to her loose end at the fringe and to an anchor inside, so she can climb back along it. She comes up into the Exchange, into her own body if it survives the crossing or into a **lawful vessel** if it does not, and is a person again in 312 AH, with her name (the Room 6 logic: name persists, so she is the same woman).

### Why now
1. **[COMMON in the Exchange]** The party brought the Stolen Shard through intake in Session 12. For the first time in three hundred years, a piece of the palace and the open claim are in the same building.
2. **[ASK]** News from Bridgehollow (via Pell's capsules): the Regarder came, the Reave vanished, Iron Bailiffs guard a black caravan, Haywards patrol the fields, people whisper about Fen-Reivers. The Thaw reads these as the Niorghast's people moving.
3. **[EARN]** Venfyura has sent word that the Goddess has noticed. Waiting no longer keeps anything secret.
4. **[EARN]** Inspector Tallis has started counting door openings. The window is closing.

## 7.2 THE METHOD (A RE-TIE, IN PLAIN TERMS)

**The picture to give the players:** Cladis's thread is a torn hem. One edge is inside the Wyrd, the other is outside, tied to a post in the Ice Palace. You mend a torn hem by sewing in a new strip and knotting it to both sides. The new strip is the line. The knots are the anchors.

| Part | What it is | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **The line** | A braided cord of three strands: burial **linen**, drawn iron **rune-wire**, and a living **fungal strand**. | It's the new length of thread. Linen holds its shape but carries nothing. Wire carries the rune's instruction but snaps in deep cold. Fungus carries a living signal but rots or freezes alone. Braided, each covers the others' weakness, the way a good rope uses strands that fail in different ways. |
| **The near knot (anchor)** | A rune-stone in the frame of the cold door, cut with **Threshold + Bind**. | A line tied at only one end just drifts. The anchor's sentence ties this end to the inside of the Wyrd: "this line belongs here", so whatever climbs it comes toward the Exchange. |
| **The far knot** | The line's far end, tied off at the gorge beside the Eirwitch camp and lowered toward the fringe. Tied by the party, with Venfyura. | Cladis's frayed end is out there. You can't knot two threads that never touch, so the new line has to be lowered to where her end is nearest: the gorge, where some frost never melts. |
| **The sinker** | Something from the palace on the far end: the **Stolen Shard** (fast) or a jar of never-melting gorge frost (slow). | A piece of a place hangs toward that place, like a plumb line. **Narrative only: the sword gains no combat property.** And the Goddess will notice it hanging there. |
| **The bell** | The camp bell, rung in Varrenmoor's **Bell** knock (four slow), steadily. | In a place where nothing changes, Cladis can't tell where the new line is. A repeating sound is the one thing that changes, so it is the one thing she can walk toward, like a climber following a camp bell in a whiteout to find the fixed rope. |
| **The vessel** | A lawful body: construct material without identity (an Exchange category), prepared like a restoration with linen, distilled water, a seed of life, a boundary rune, needle and thread, and identity anchors. | She shredded her own thread to get out. What comes back along a mended thread may be only herself: name and mind. The Ossuary already puts people into bodies that aren't their first; this is the same work done carefully and legally. If her own body makes it, the vessel is simply not used. |
| **The three records** | Intake ledger (her name), custody ledger (the "held" line), and the **physical tag**, outstanding for 300 years. | The Exchange's threshold only accepts a named person into a body when all three agree. That is the safety catch: anything else that follows the bell (a Nox, an echo, something wearing her voice) doesn't match all three, so the threshold won't take it. |

**Where each end is**
- **Near end:** the **cold door**, an old iron service door behind Pell's dusty terminal. The ledger lists it as permanently sealed (just as the Eirwitch passage was, in Session 12). Its old rune opens it for **one minute** when the last trolley of the night passes. That minute is "the usual time". On the other side: a snowy mountain road above Bridgehollow, a short walk from the Eirwitch camp. **NEW**
- **Far end:** the river gorge beside the camp. **[ASK]** Pell: *"Cold enough there that some frost never melts. That's how you know the edge is close."* Venfyura keeps the fire burning by the stave church because a fire is the opposite of the palace: it is always changing.

**The order of operations (for the later session):** the party carries the far end out, recovers the clapper, gets Venfyura's help, rings the Bell knock, lowers the line on its sinker, and ties it off at the gorge. When Cladis takes hold, Violet's living strand tells her. The Thaw checks the three records at the moment of arrival, and the threshold accepts Cladis. **[UNSETTLED]** whether it works, and what Lakkar asks in return, is for Session 15 or later.

## 7.3 THE CRAFT TASKS (WHY NOBODY CAN DO THIS ALONE)

**Craft rules recap (from the handoff).** Craft check = `1d20 + proficiency + craft ability` against the scenario DC. At level 4: maximum 3 charges per craft, long rest restores all. Free analysis costs nothing. A trainer's supervision on a task gives **advantage on one check** (help, not extra power). **Tier I = DC 12, Tier II = DC 14** in this section.

**Dependencies at a glance**
| Task | Needs first | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| T1 Near knot (Stellan) | Clean wire from T2 (or disadvantage) | The test pull (T6) |
| T2 Wire and frost (Dale) | Nothing | T1 material, T3 survival, proof the gorge is the place |
| T3 Living strand (Violet) | Rime dose from T2 (or the strand freezes in an hour) | The signal for T6 and for the real night |
| T4 Vessel (Betsy) | Nothing | A Stable vessel; Ysolde stops worrying |
| T5 Third record (party) | The open claim from Sabeline or Pringus | The records agree; Tallis can be talked down |
| T6 Braid and test pull (Europa or anyone) | T1, T2, T3 | Proof it works, the capsule and the name, and the way out |

### T1. THE NEAR KNOT (Stellan, Runecrafting) **Tier II**
- **Where:** the cold door (Scenes spec B3). Quill supervises.
- **Read first:** Read Inscription (free, DC 13) on the anchor stone. Properties in order: (1) a two-rune sentence: **subject** the line, **Threshold + Bind**, **scope** this door, **condition** while the bell sounds, **anchor** this stone; (2) it is **Fraying**: someone repaired it with the wrong iron; (3, +5) the bad iron carries a trace of **Hunger**, which slowly eats the instruction. *(Quill, quietly: "Hunger. Her name has that word in it. I don't like that either.")*
- **Do:** Repair Line twice (1 charge each, 1 minute each), DC 14. Fraying → Strained → Stable. The clean wire from T2 counts as suitable material; the old wire is knowingly unsuitable (**disadvantage**, per the Repair Line rule).
- **Success:** the stone hums faintly when anyone taps four slow near it. The knot is Stable.
- **Failure:** worsens one step and backlash: the door's open minute **drifts** (1d6 x 5 minutes later tonight) and Stellan takes 1d6 cold. Fail forward: Quill re-cuts the worst stroke overnight; Strained still works, with a risk later.
- **If Stellan doesn't know Threshold or Bind:** the Threshold glyph is cut plainly into the door's lintel. Ten minutes of study with Quill counts as discovering and studying it. If Quill has not yet given the Quest 1 reward, this *is* that reward (one Tier I glyph, no extra charges).

### T2. THE WIRE AND THE FROST (Dale, Essencecrafting) **Tier I, one Tier II check**
- **Where:** Brokka's forge. Brokka grumbles and lends the forge; not in on it.
- **Pick the iron:** four bars from Haddo's stock. Analyze (free; one careful 10 minutes covers all four, DC 12): **bar 1** Iron, Potency 1, Stable (clean); **bars 2 and 3** Iron with a **Hunger** trace (salvage from an old Mwooreth fitting; this is what has been eating the anchor); **bar 4** Stone. Draw the clean bar into wire: smith's tools DC 12 (Dale or anyone, with Brokka's advantage).
- **Match the frost (Tier II):** Analyze the frost on the Stolen Shard and the jar of gorge frost from Pell, DC 14. Success: both are **Rime**, Potency 1, and +5 gives one more property: *"it never fully melts"*. This is the party's own proof that the gorge is where Cladis's end is nearest. Dale gets no time essence (canon).
- **Dose:** Extract a Rime dose from the jar (Stable, so automatic; 10 minutes; free at rest, otherwise 1 charge), then Apply Essence to the braid (1 charge + the dose): **preserves organic matter 24 hours**, so the linen and the fungus survive the cold.
- **Failure:** a wrong bar gets used. T1 is at disadvantage until someone notices (Insight or Dale's next Analyze). Nothing explodes.

### T3. THE LIVING STRAND (Violet, Starweaving of Root and Spore) **Tier I**
- **Where:** Ysolde's restoration house, where the culture is kept in a glass case.
- **Read:** Read Green Pattern (free, DC 12): a cave mycelium. Vitality **Strained**. Affliction: **cold blocking growth**. Network: none yet.
- **Grow:** Quickening (1 charge, 1 minute concentration, about a week's growth) on the culture. DC 13 because the culture is Strained. Success: a 30-ft strand, enough to braid. Failure: 10 ft; a second Quickening (another charge) finishes it.
- **Tie:** Tie Green Thread (1 charge, 10 minutes) between the culture case (it stays at the near knot) and the strand's far tip. While the strand is touched, Violet feels direction and Vitality within 1 mile. **That is the signal**: on the real night, when something takes hold at the far end, the strand's Vitality changes and Violet knows.
- **Violet's thread:** Pell mentions the culture came from "the woman at the fire". **[EARN]** If Violet asks who, Pell won't say. If she has met Venfyura, Read Green Pattern shows the culture was tended by the same careful hand as her grandmother's chart (her call; this is the first breadcrumb, not a reveal).
- **Limit check (canon):** Violet's craft reads the fungus only. She cannot read Cladis, the vessel, or anyone who holds the line.

### T4. THE VESSEL'S FIT (Betsy, Splicing) **Tier II**
- **Where:** Ysolde's vessel room. Pimm may be fetched to supervise (Pimm is not in on it; Ysolde tells Pimm it is "a cold-climate restoration").
- **The problem:** whatever climbs back along the line arrives carrying the palace's cold *inside* it. An ordinary body would freeze from the core outward. The vessel's grown tissue (started from the seed of life on a lawful construct frame) needs a viable cold adaptation.
- **The choice (Pimm's rule: a problem, a continuation, a cost):** Ysolde offers three continuations. Betsy (Medicine or Nature DC 12 to judge, free) picks one:
  - **Warm from the core** (a deep layer of heat-making fat around the heart and spine, as some hibernating animals have). **Viable and correct.** Cost: the body is always hungry.
  - **Cold-proof blood that slows down** (like a frog that freezes in winter). Viable, but wrong problem: the body would survive the cold by stopping, which is exactly where she has been stuck. Ysolde points this out if chosen.
  - **"Frost-proof bones."** Not a viable continuation. Pimm: *"That is not biology. That is a wish."*
- **Do:** apply the chosen continuation with Betsy's Splicing as her sheet defines it. **Her sheet is authoritative** (the handoff says not to substitute the other crafts' pool). Suggested check DC 14, advantage with Pimm supervising.
- **Success:** the vessel is **Stable**. **Failure:** **Strained**. Return still possible, but if the vessel is used she arrives weak and cold. Fail forward.

### T5. THE THIRD RECORD (whole party: Investigation, History, Persuasion; Pringus)
- **The problem:** intake and custody agree. The **physical tag** has been "outstanding" for 300 years.
- **Where the tag is:** Cladis left it on her trail, among the **old intake tags** in the chest and passage material from Session 12 (the handoff lists "old intake tags" in the passage guide). **[UNSETTLED]** Check whether the party took them. If yes, one is already in someone's pack: Investigation DC 12 to spot a tag stamped with the Winter crown's mark and an intake number matching Sabeline's page. If no, it is still in the passage under the Cladis memorial (a short trip; History DC 12 to read the old numbering).
- **Second path:** the **Rite of the Remaining Bone** (Expose Claim, 10 minutes, a copper coin, no charges) on the tag shows the claim it belongs to.
- **Make it agree:** Pringus runs a **Reconciliation Audit** if a party member with custody presents the tag. *"I know this tag is real. The ledger states the claim is open. I infer it was left to be found. I do not know what is at the other end."* Once he notes that all three agree, the claim is **reconciled** and the threshold will accept the named person.
- **The catch:** an audit is a public record. Inspector Tallis reads audits (see 7.6). Doing it honestly makes the operation legal and visible. Skipping it makes the operation invisible and impossible.

### T6. THE BRAID AND THE TEST PULL (Europa, or anyone) **skill task**
- **Where:** Haddo's ropewalk, then the cold door at the usual time. Nan Gristle lends the trolley signal bell.
- **Braid:** Haddo braids; someone has to hold tension and keep count while three strands of different stiffness go together. Dexterity (weaver's tools or Sleight of Hand) DC 12. Failure: a kink; Haddo redoes it (20 minutes lost).
- **The count:** at the cold door, someone rings Nan's bell in the Bell knock (four slow) while Violet holds the strand and Stellan watches the anchor. The rhythm must be exact. **Europa** is ideal: an innkeeper's daughter who calmed rooms with a little magic, and her Clockwork Soul's sense of order. Performance or Wisdom DC 12 (advantage for Europa if her sheet has a Clockwork feature that fits; your call). A wrong rhythm makes the strand go slack and nothing happens.
- **Success:** the cold door opens for its minute. The braid's tip goes through. Violet feels the strand cool and steady: the line holds on the far side. A capsule drops through, and the Goddess's notice arrives (see Scene 7).
- **Lil' Apostrophe:** Lil wants to write BELL on the bell. If allowed, roll Part 3. If the result is anything loud, the test still works, and Tallis hears it from two corridors away.

**How they tell it (in layers, see 7.7):**
1. [ASK] Sabeline: *"There is an open claim in my ledger. It has been open for three hundred years. I would like to close it properly."*
2. [ASK] She shows the copied custody page: "Cladis. Named. Held: the place where even time cannot leave. Tag: outstanding." Anyone who has read the sword sees the same words. **(Clue E.)**
