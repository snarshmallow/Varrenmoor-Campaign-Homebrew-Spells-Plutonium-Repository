## 7.0 OVERVIEW (THE ONE-PAGE VERSION)

**The goal of the session.** The party finds out that Quill belongs to a small, careful group inside the Exchange, the Thaw, that is building a way to bring **Cladis**, the last known Winter Queen, back. The party helps build the Ossuary end of it with their crafts. At the end of the night they learn the last piece can only be finished **outside**, at the Eirwitch camp in the mountains above **Bridgehollow**, and they leave through an old door that opens onto that mountain road.

**What the party learns**
1. Cladis is not dead in any way the Exchange recognizes. Her name sits on an **open claim** that has been copied forward in the custody ledger for about three hundred years. The custody line says she is held at "the place where even time cannot leave", the same words cut into the Stolen Shard. **NEW**
2. That place is the Rhime Goddess's Ice Palace at the Wyrd's fringe. Nothing there changes, so Cladis is not suffering and not aging. She is *stopped*, like a clock with its hand held. **NEW (user-directed)**
3. The Thaw wants her back because the signs say the **Niorghast** is moving (the Regarder in Bridgehollow, the Fen-Reiver rumors), and the Winter office existed for exactly this: existential threats, final authority, invoked in crisis.
4. **The method** is a plain, buildable thing: a **line** she can follow, a **bell** that tells her which way to go, an **anchor** that holds the near end, a **vessel** (a lawful body) to step into, and **three agreeing records** so the Exchange's own threshold will accept her and nothing else.
5. The far end of the line has to be lowered into the gorge beside the Eirwitch camp, and the bell has to be rung there. The camp bell's clapper has been seized by Bridgehollow's new authorities.

**What it costs**
- Most of the day's craft charges (see 7.4; about 2 per crafter).
- Risk: Inspector Tallis of the Exchange is close to noticing. If he files his notice, the cold door is sealed for good and the party walks the long way.
- The Stolen Shard is needed (shown, measured, later used as the line's sinker). It stays in party custody, but the party has to decide to carry it into the riskiest place it has been since it was stolen.
- A promise. The Thaw asks for silence. Breaking it in a common hall gets the cell's members fired or worse, and the Thaw stops trusting the party.

**How it ends.** At "the usual time" the cold door opens for one minute. A capsule comes through from the mountain side: *"Bell silent. Clapper taken to the Customs House. Bailiffs on the road."* The Thaw can finish the Ossuary end. Only the party can finish the far end. The door will not open again if Tallis files tomorrow. They go now.

### What Cladis is, and where
- **[ASK] (canon)** Cladis is the last known Winter Queen. She led the final advance on Nior's fortress. The record stops when she goes in; a precise Mwooreth cut removed the next sentence.
- **[EARN] NEW** The Exchange holds an **open claim** under her name. The intake ledger lists her as *named*. The custody ledger, copied forward by hand every few decades, says: *"Held: the place where even time cannot leave. Tag: outstanding."* Nobody alive wrote that line.
- **[EARN] NEW** "The place where even time cannot leave" is the Rhime Goddess's Ice Palace at the Wyrd's fringe. That is where the Stolen Shard came from. So the sword and the claim point at the same place.

### What "return" means
Not resurrection. Cladis is not dead; she is stopped. Return means she **walks out** of the stopped place along a line, comes up into a **lawful body** waiting at the Ossuary end, and is a person again in 312 AH, with her name and her memories (up to the moment she stopped). Like Wren in Room 6: *name persists, so she is the same woman.*

### Why now
1. **[COMMON in the Exchange]** The party brought the Stolen Shard through intake in Session 12. The custody ledger now lists it. Sabeline saw the entry. For the first time in three hundred years, a piece of the palace and the claim are in the same building.
2. **[ASK]** News from Bridgehollow (via the courier capsules): the Regarder came, the Reave vanished, Iron Bailiffs guard a black caravan, Haywards patrol the fields, people whisper about Fen-Reivers. The Thaw reads these as the Niorghast's people moving.
3. **[EARN]** Inspector Tallis has started counting door openings. The window is closing.

## 7.2 THE METHOD (HOW IT ACTUALLY WORKS)

Read these to the players as the Thaw explains them. Each is 2 to 3 plain sentences, with the "why".

| Part | What it is | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **The bell** | The camp bell, rung in the Varrenmoor **Bell** knock (four slow), steady, for as long as it takes. | In a place where nothing changes, you can't tell which way to go. A sound that keeps repeating is the one thing that changes, so it is the one thing she can walk toward, the way a climber in a whiteout follows the camp bell. |
| **The line** | A braided cord of three strands: burial **linen**, drawn iron **rune-wire**, and a living **fungal strand**. | Linen holds its shape but carries nothing. Wire carries the rune's instruction but snaps in deep cold. Fungus carries a living signal but rots or freezes alone. Braided, each covers the others' weakness, the way a good rope uses strands that fail in different ways. |
| **The sinker** | Something from the palace, tied to the far end and lowered into the gorge: the **Stolen Shard** (fast) or a jar of the gorge's never-melting frost (slow). | A piece of a place sits better in that place than anywhere else, so the line hangs toward the palace the way a plumb line hangs toward the ground. **NEW, narrative only: this gives the sword no combat property.** |
| **The anchor** | A rune-stone set in the frame of the cold door, holding the near end. | A line tied at only one end just drifts. The anchor's sentence tells the door "this line belongs here", so pulling on the line pulls toward the Exchange, not away from it. |
| **The vessel** | A lawful body: construct material without identity (an Exchange category), prepared like a restoration with linen, distilled water, a seed of life, a boundary rune, needle and thread, and identity anchors. | Cladis may come back as only herself: name and mind, no body (her body's fate was cut out of the record). The Ossuary already puts people into bodies that aren't their first; this is the same work, done carefully and legally. |
| **The three records** | Intake ledger (her name), custody ledger (the "held" line), and the **physical tag**, which has been "outstanding" for 300 years. | The Exchange's threshold only moves a named person into a body when all three agree. That rule is the safety catch: anything else that follows the bell (a Nox, an echo, a hollow thing wearing her voice) does not match all three records, so the threshold will not take it. |

**Where each end is**
- **Near end:** the **cold door**, an old iron service door behind Pell's dusty terminal. The ledger lists it as permanently sealed (just as the Eirwitch passage was, in Session 12). Its old rune opens it for **one minute** when the last trolley of the night passes. That minute is "the usual time". On the other side: a snowy mountain road above Bridgehollow, a short walk from the Eirwitch camp. **NEW**
- **Far end:** the river gorge beside the Eirwitch camp. **[ASK]** Pell: *"Cold enough there that some frost never melts. That's how you know the edge is close."* The old woman keeps the fire burning by the stave church because the fire is the opposite of the palace: it is always changing.

**The order of operations (for the later session):** the party carries the far end out, recovers the clapper, gets the old woman's consent, rings the Bell knock, lowers the line on its sinker, and Violet's living strand tells the Ossuary end when something takes hold. The Thaw checks the three records at the moment of arrival, and the threshold accepts her into the vessel. **[UNSETTLED]** Whether it works, and what comes up the line, is yours to decide in Session 15 or later.

## 7.3 THE CRAFT TASKS (WHY NOBODY CAN DO THIS ALONE)

**Craft rules recap (from the handoff).** Craft check = `1d20 + proficiency + craft ability` against the scenario DC. At level 4: maximum 3 charges per craft, long rest restores all. Free analysis costs nothing. A trainer's supervision on a task gives **advantage on one check** (help, not extra power). **Tier I = DC 12, Tier II = DC 14** in this section.

**Dependencies at a glance**
| Task | Needs first | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| T1 Anchor (Stellan) | Clean wire from T2 (or disadvantage) | The test pull (T6) |
| T2 Wire and frost (Dale) | Nothing | T1 material, T3 survival, proof the gorge is the place |
| T3 Living strand (Violet) | Rime dose from T2 (or the strand freezes in an hour) | The signal for T6 and for the real night |
| T4 Vessel (Betsy) | Nothing | A Stable vessel; Ysolde stops worrying |
| T5 Third record (party) | The open claim from Sabeline or Pringus | The records agree; Tallis can be talked down |
| T6 Braid and test pull (Europa or anyone) | T1, T2, T3 | Proof it works, and the capsule that sends them out |

### T1. THE ANCHOR (Stellan, Runecrafting) **Tier II**
- **Where:** the cold door (Scenes spec 3). Quill supervises.
- **Read first:** Read Inscription (free, DC 13) on the anchor stone. Properties in order: (1) it is a two-rune sentence: **subject** the line, **Threshold + Bind**, **scope** this door, **condition** while the bell sounds, **anchor** this stone; (2) it is **Fraying**: someone repaired it with the wrong iron; (3, +5) the bad iron carries a trace of **Hunger**, which slowly eats the instruction.
- **Do:** Repair Line twice (1 charge each, 1 minute each), DC 14. Fraying → Strained → Stable. Using the clean wire from T2 counts as suitable material; the old wire is knowingly unsuitable (**disadvantage**, per the Repair Line rule).
- **Success:** the stone hums faintly when anyone taps four slow near it. The anchor is Stable.
- **Failure:** worsens one step and backlash: the door's open minute **drifts** (roll 1d6 x 5 minutes later tonight) and Stellan takes 1d6 cold. Fail forward: Quill re-cuts the worst stroke overnight, and the anchor is Strained, which still works, with a risk later.
- **If Stellan doesn't know Threshold or Bind:** the Threshold glyph is cut plainly into the door's lintel. Ten minutes of study with Quill counts as discovering and studying it. If Quill has not yet given the Quest 1 reward, this *is* that reward (one Tier I glyph, no extra charges).

### T2. THE WIRE AND THE FROST (Dale, Essencecrafting) **Tier I, one Tier II check**
- **Where:** Brokka's forge. Brokka grumbles and lends the forge; not in on it.
- **Pick the iron:** four bars from Haddo's stock. Analyze (free; one careful 10 minutes covers all four, DC 12): **bar 1** Iron, Potency 1, Stable (clean); **bars 2 and 3** Iron with a **Hunger** trace (salvage from an old Mwooreth fitting; this is what has been eating the anchor); **bar 4** Stone. Draw the clean bar into wire: smith's tools DC 12 (Dale or anyone, with Brokka's advantage).
- **Match the frost (Tier II):** Analyze the frost on the Stolen Shard and the jar of gorge frost from Pell, DC 14. Success: both are **Rime**, Potency 1, and +5 reveals one more property: *"it never fully melts"*. This is the party's own proof that the gorge is the right place. Dale learns nothing about frozen time and gets no time essence (canon).
- **Dose:** Extract a Rime dose from the jar (Stable, so automatic; 10 minutes; free at rest, otherwise 1 charge), then Apply Essence to the braid (1 charge + the dose): **preserves organic matter 24 hours**, so the linen and the fungus survive the cold.
- **Failure:** a wrong bar gets used. T1 is at disadvantage until someone notices (Insight or Dale's next Analyze). Nothing explodes.

### T3. THE LIVING STRAND (Violet, Starweaving of Root and Spore) **Tier I**
- **Where:** Ysolde's restoration house, where the culture is kept in a glass case.
- **Read:** Read Green Pattern (free, DC 12): a cave mycelium. Vitality **Strained**. Affliction: **cold blocking growth**. Network: none yet.
- **Grow:** Quickening (1 charge, 1 minute concentration, about a week's growth) on the culture. DC 13 because the culture is Strained. Success: a 30-ft strand, enough to braid. Failure: 10 ft; a second Quickening (another charge) finishes it.
- **Tie:** Tie Green Thread (1 charge, 10 minutes) between the culture case (it stays at the anchor) and the strand's far tip. While the strand is touched, Violet feels direction and Vitality within 1 mile. **That is the signal**: on the real night, when something takes hold at the far end, the strand's Vitality changes and Violet knows.
- **Limit check (canon):** Violet's craft reads the fungus only. She cannot read Cladis, the vessel, or anyone who holds the line.

### T4. THE VESSEL'S FIT (Betsy, Splicing) **Tier II**
- **Where:** Ysolde's vessel room. Pimm may be fetched to supervise (Pimm is not in on it; Ysolde tells Pimm it is "a cold-climate restoration").
- **The problem:** whatever comes up the line arrives carrying the palace's cold *inside* it. An ordinary body would freeze from the core outward. The vessel's grown tissue (started from the seed of life on a lawful construct frame) needs a viable cold adaptation.
- **The choice (Pimm's rule: a problem, a continuation, a cost):** Ysolde offers three continuations. Betsy (Medicine or Nature DC 12 to judge, free) picks one:
  - **Warm from the core** (a deep layer of heat-making fat around the heart and spine, as some hibernating animals have). **Viable and correct.** Cost: the body is always hungry.
  - **Cold-proof blood that slows down** (like a frog that freezes in winter). Viable, but wrong problem: the body would survive the cold by stopping, which is exactly what she is escaping. Ysolde points this out if chosen.
  - **"Frost-proof bones."** Not a viable continuation. Pimm: *"That is not biology. That is a wish."*
- **Do:** apply the chosen continuation with Betsy's Splicing as her sheet defines it. **Her sheet is authoritative** (the handoff says not to substitute the other crafts' pool). Suggested check DC 14, advantage with Pimm supervising.
- **Success:** the vessel is **Stable**. **Failure:** **Strained**. Return still possible, but she will arrive weak and cold (yours to play later). Fail forward.

### T5. THE THIRD RECORD (whole party: Investigation, History, Persuasion; Pringus)
- **The problem:** intake and custody agree. The **physical tag** has been "outstanding" for 300 years.
- **Where the tag is:** among the **old intake tags** in the chest and passage material from Session 12 (the handoff lists "old intake tags" in the passage guide). **[UNSETTLED]** Check whether the party took them. If yes, one is already in someone's pack: Investigation DC 12 to spot a tag stamped with the Winter crown's mark and an intake number matching Sabeline's page. If no, it is still in the passage under the Cladis memorial (a short trip; History DC 12 to read the old numbering).
- **Second path:** the **Rite of the Remaining Bone** (Expose Claim, 10 minutes, a copper coin, no charges) on the tag shows the claim it belongs to.
- **Make it agree:** Pringus runs a **Reconciliation Audit** if a party member with custody presents the tag. *"I know this tag is real. The ledger states the claim is open. I infer it has been carried forward by people too tired to close it. I do not know what is at the other end."* Once he has noted that all three agree, the claim is **reconciled** and the threshold will accept the named person into a lawful vessel.
- **The catch:** an audit is a public record. Inspector Tallis reads audits (see 7.6). Doing it honestly makes the operation legal and visible. Skipping it makes the operation invisible and impossible.

### T6. THE BRAID AND THE TEST PULL (Europa, or anyone) **skill task**
- **Where:** Haddo's ropewalk, then the cold door at the usual time. Nan Gristle lends the trolley signal bell.
- **Braid:** Haddo braids; someone has to hold tension and keep count while three strands of different stiffness go together. Dexterity (weaver's tools or Sleight of Hand) DC 12. Failure: a kink; Haddo redoes it (20 minutes lost).
- **The count:** at the cold door, someone rings Nan's bell in the Bell knock (four slow) while Violet holds the strand and Stellan watches the anchor. The rhythm must be exact. **Europa** is ideal: an innkeeper's daughter who calmed rooms with a little magic, and her Clockwork Soul's sense of order. Performance or Wisdom DC 12 (advantage for Europa if her sheet has a Clockwork feature that fits; your call). A wrong rhythm makes the strand go slack and nothing happens.
- **Success:** the cold door opens for its minute. The braid's tip goes through. Violet feels the strand cool and steady: the line holds on the far side. A capsule drops through (see Scene 7).
- **Lil' Apostrophe:** Lil wants to write BELL on the bell. If allowed, roll Part 3. If the result is anything loud, the test still works, and Tallis hears it from two corridors away.
