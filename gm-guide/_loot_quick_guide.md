
### 6.0b LOOT QUICK GUIDE (every clue item, where it is, what it is for)
Each is a loot item in Foundry (folder *Quest Items (Side Quests)*) with a small 3D prop and a player-facing description only; the notes below are GM-only.

| Item | Where it sits | Quest | What it is for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rune Slip signed "Q."** | Suite bedroom 3 desk (upstairs) | 1 | Opens Quest 1. Stellan sees only a "Q." and doodles that look like pieces of runes: he recognizes rune fragments but cannot place the hand. The initial is the lead (ask around: Quill). |
| **Tin: "S. Rattus, Custodian (Acting)"** | Room 207, by the bed | 1 | The dead rat with a real custodian tag. Leads to Dunmore Kell; the intake and custody ledgers disagree about it. |
| **Notice: Confirm Authorization (Warehouse Door)** | Tavern notices desk | 1 | Tells the party the warehouse door now "believes" a rat is its custodian. |
| **Pringus's Ledger Excerpt: The Rat** | Not placed (Pringus or a clerk gives it) | 1 | Shows intake and custody disagree ("two is a rumor"). Sets up the paper fix. |
| **Chewed Clinic Tag (Pimm's Mark)** | Suite bedroom 1, by a bed | 2 | First clue that Gerald (Pimm's patient) is loose in the Exchange. |
| **Doctor Pimm's Field Notebook** | Gerald's nest shelf, Lost Property annex | 2 | The quest goal. Pimm's adaptation rules; holds the loose page below. |
| **Loose Page: Animal-Holding Box** | Not placed (inside the notebook) | 2 | A breadcrumb about Betsy's lineage. Unresolved; Betsy keeps it. |
| **Lost Property Tag: Unclaimed After Notice** | Annex ledger table | 2 | Flavor: shows what "unclaimed after notice" means. Every tag is long past its date. |
| **Suite Guest Book** | Suite living room, bookcase | 3 | An old entry mentions "the man down the hall in six." Points at Room 6. |
| **Brass Plate "6"** | Corridor wall by the room 206 door | 3 | Marks Room 6 as older than the rest. Pigeonhole bills pile up outside. |
| **Overdue Lodging Bill (43 Years)** | Tavern notices desk | 3 | The bill Wren disputes. Hook for his story. |
| **Bone Tag (Wren's Identity Anchor)** | Floor of room 206 | 3 | Perception DC 12. His original anchor; a Witness memory confirms a continuous mind. |
| **Wren's Stamped Referral Card** | Not placed (Wren hands it over) | 3 | Quest reward: sets up Bones's restoration without guaranteeing it. |
| **Capsule: "Drop off at the usual time."** | Plinth at the dusty terminal | Note lead | The unsolved lead: arrives at the same hour, bone-style tag and a letter. Never tie it to Gerald, Kell or Wren. |
