# Part 7: (Session 14) The Thaw

> **Current campaign clarifications.** Cladis is stopped, not dead: the Exchange holds an open claim for her, "held: the place where even time cannot leave" (the Rhime Goddess's Ice Palace). The Thaw is a small secret group in the Exchange, and Quill is in it. Nobody knows what happened inside Nior's fortress or who cut the sentence. The sword and the egg stay in party custody. Trainers give no extra charges. Werd is not Wyrd.

## Overview

**Goal.** The party discovers that Quill belongs to the Thaw, a careful group building a way to bring Cladis, the last known Winter Queen, back. They help build the Ossuary end with their crafts. At the end of the night they learn the last piece can only be finished outside, at the Eirwitch camp above Bridgehollow, and they leave through an old door that opens onto that mountain road.

**What the party learns**

- Cladis's name sits on an open claim copied forward in the custody ledger for about 300 years: "Held: the place where even time cannot leave. Tag: outstanding." Those are the words on the Stolen Shard. (NEW)
- That place is the Rhime Goddess's Ice Palace. Nothing there changes, so Cladis is stopped, like a clock with its hand held. (NEW)
- The Thaw wants her back because the Niorghast's people are moving around Bridgehollow, and the Winter office exists for exactly this.
- The method is buildable: a bell to walk toward, a line to hold, an anchor, a lawful body, and three agreeing records.
- The far end must be lowered into the gorge by the Eirwitch camp, and the camp bell's clapper has been seized in Bridgehollow.

**What it costs.** Most of a day's craft charges; the risk that Inspector Tallis seals the door for good; the Stolen Shard is needed (it stays in party custody); and a promise of silence.

**How it ends.** At "the usual time" the cold door opens for one minute. A capsule arrives: "Bell silent. Clapper taken to the Customs House. Bailiffs on the road." After noon tomorrow the door will not open again. They go now.

**What "return" means.** Not resurrection. She walks out of the stopped place along a line, steps into a lawful body at the Ossuary end, and is herself again in 312 AH. Name persists, so she is the same woman (the Room 6 logic).

## The method

| Part | What it is | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| The bell | The camp bell rung in the Bell knock (four slow), steadily | Where nothing changes, a repeating sound is the one thing that changes, so it is the one thing she can walk toward, like a climber following a camp bell in a whiteout. |
| The line | A braid of burial linen, iron rune-wire and a living fungal strand | Linen holds shape but carries nothing; wire carries the instruction but snaps in cold; fungus carries a living signal but rots alone. Braided, each covers the others. |
| The sinker | The Stolen Shard (fast) or a jar of the gorge's never-melting frost (slow) | A piece of a place hangs toward that place, like a plumb line. Narrative only: the sword gains no power. |
| The anchor | A rune-stone in the cold door's frame | A line tied at one end drifts. The anchor tells the door "this line belongs here", so pulling brings things toward the Exchange. |
| The vessel | A lawful body: construct material without identity, prepared like a restoration | She may come back as name and mind only. The Ossuary already puts people into bodies; this is the same work done legally. |
| Three records | Intake (her name), custody (the "held" line), and the physical tag | The threshold only accepts a named person into a body when all three agree. Anything else following the bell (a Nox, an echo) won't match, so it can't come through. |

**The cold door.** An old iron service door behind Pell's dusty terminal, recorded as permanently sealed. Its rune opens it for one minute when the last trolley passes ("the usual time"). The other side is a snowy road above Bridgehollow, near the camp. (NEW)

**The far end.** The gorge beside the camp, where some frost never melts. The old woman keeps the fire burning because a fire is always changing: the opposite of the palace.

- **T4.** Ysolde offers three continuations: warm from the core (correct; cost: always hungry), freeze-and-stop blood (wrong problem: she is escaping stopping), frost-proof bones (not viable: "That is a wish").
- **T5.** The tag is among the old intake tags from the Session 12 passage ([UNSETTLED] whether the party took them). Pringus: "I know this tag is real. The ledger states the claim is open. I infer it has been carried forward by people too tired to close it. I do not know what is at the other end."

## If the authorities notice

- **Inside the Exchange:** Tallis posts a notice of irregularity and the door is sealed by Exchange order. Each delayed night moves him a step: questions, then Sabeline's contract under review (staff can be reassigned, not bought), then the notice regardless. The Exchange closes doors and accounts; that is all, and enough.
- **Outside:** public notices are read by people who report to the Regarder. Iron Bailiffs already hold the clapper at the Customs House; after a notice, Haywards patrol the mountain road too. Fen-Reivers stay rumor: tracks, a missing goat, claw marks in snow. A Verderant stays a story.

*"Rope goes where it's tied. I tie it. That's the end of my curiosity."*

**Snapshot.** Big cheerful man with two donors' arms, one old and scarred, one young and freckled. Braids the line; knows nothing else. Sold the party's bad iron by accident. **Voice.** Slow, literal, measures in arm-lengths. **Where.** The Ropewalk.

### Pell Ostry, Keeper of the Dusty Terminal

*"Usual time. Single clunk. Never early."*

**Snapshot.** Legless sentient skeleton who rides a counterweighted chair on a short rail. Keeps the cold door's timetable and the Thaw's capsules. Has a jar of gorge frost. **Voice.** Short, precise, dry; checks a watch with no hands. **Where.** The Dusty Terminal.

- [ASK] "Usual time is when the last trolley passes. One minute. Not two."
- [EARN, Europa only] "The man who carried you in came through my door. Said to tell you the time is right, and he's at the fire."

### Corvin Tallis, Inspector of Thresholds (pressure)

*"Is the door sealed? The ledger says it is. My book says it opens. One of these is wrong."*

**Snapshot.** Tall, living human in a grey uniform with a brass measuring chain. Checks that every door does what the ledger says. Has counted forty-one openings. Not a villain: he wants the Exchange honest. **Voice.** Polite, precise, one-answer questions. **Where.** The tenth-hour trolley; the Night Custody Office.

- [COMMON] "I don't arrest anyone. I close doors. It's usually enough."
- [EARN] "If the three records agree, what passes is a lawful transfer, and it's my job to let it. I hate that you've made this my job."

### Nan Gristle, Trolley Signalwoman (ally, not in on it)

**Snapshot.** Loud, kind old half-orc with a brass speaking-trumpet and a signal bell. Loved the day Lil's ducks rode her trolleys. Lends the bell and can delay Tallis's trolley. **Voice.** Shouts through the trumpet even up close. **Where.** The Signal Loft above the Ropewalk.
