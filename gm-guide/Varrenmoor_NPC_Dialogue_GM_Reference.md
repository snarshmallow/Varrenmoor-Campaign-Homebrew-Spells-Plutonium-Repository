# VARRENMOOR GM REFERENCE: NPC VOICES, ANSWERS & LORE
*Written for use at the table, Session 14 onward. Current year: 312 AH. Party is in the Lower Ossuary market.*

## HOW TO USE THIS
Each fact and answer carries a tag so you never spoil something by accident.

- **[COMMON]** Anyone local would say this freely.
- **[ASK]** They'll share it if asked politely or if it's relevant.
- **[EARN]** They share it only after a favor, a payment, a good Insight/Persuasion roll, or trust.
- **[SECRET]** DM only. Listed so you know what *not* to let slip. Items marked **[UNSETTLED]** are open in your canon, so decide before you use them.
- Lines in *italics* are stage direction.
- Dialogue is flavor. The facts under it come from your handoff and the history chart. Where canon is silent, the guide says so rather than inventing.

**Standing rules from your handoff**
- Mwooreth speech sounds alien but is *ordinary in-world*. NPCs never comment on it.
- **Werd** (Lil's vocabulary magic) is not **Wyrd** (the cosmic structure).
- Never let anyone state the full four-PC convergence or the cause of the Great Tangle.
- Don't silently move the sword or the egg out of party custody.

# PART 1: LORE CHEAT SHEET

## 1.1 Calendar and the Hanging
- **[COMMON]** Years are counted **AH, After the Hanging**. The Allfather was hanged; the Church teaches this as a martyrdom. The year is **312 AH**.
- **[ASK]** *"The Church is a shell."* **Alvar** used the martyr story to *protect* the Old Ways, building the Church (1–15 AH) as a cover that hid and preserved them. Few living people know this.
- **[SECRET]** The truth of the Hanging itself is **unresolved** in your canon. No NPC knows what really happened. Everyone has a version.

## 1.2 The Four Divine Relationships (the Old Ways)
- **Eirwyn** covers preservation, life, healing and memory. Water, ice, light, weaving. Keepers: **Eirwitches**.
- **Ikkander** covers order, patterns, threads and weaving. Metal, earth, steam, industry, alchemy.
- **Vaelweyr** covers fate, souls, stars and possibility. Primeval beasts, living-world magic.
- **Mwooreth** covers severance, fraying, cuts and endings. Darkness, curses, black fire.

> The campaign handoff and the chart phrase some domains differently, so use whichever fits the scene. **Mwooreth is not "the evil one."** It is *severance*, a necessary force that was weaponized. Mwooreth practitioners existed before the conquest, and some were persecuted too.

## 1.3 Mwooreth: History and the War
Timeline (BH = Before the Hanging):
- **c.120–100 BH.** **Nior** is born, poor and human. His family's hearth-seam imagery marks him early.
- **c.90–70 BH.** First Black Frost. Nior's diary shows an obsession with loss, mercy, death and thresholds.
- **c.65–45 BH.** Nior's dangerous work on the *spaces between* strands of the Wyrd. He leaves hollow echoes behind.
- **c.45–35 BH.** **Alvar** is born into a true, uncorrupted Mwooreth priestly line.
- **c.35 BH.** Mwooreth expansion begins as territory, then turns **ideological**.
- **c.25 BH.** Persecution intensifies. Eirwyn, Ikkander, Vaelweyr and independent Mwooreth practitioners are hunted. Shrines, specialists and records are destroyed.
- **c.20–5 BH.** **The Great Concealment.** Alvar and others hide rites, prayers, names, funerary customs and knowledge inside new forms.
- **0 AH.** The Hanging of the Allfather.
- **1–15 AH.** The Church is formed as a hiding place.
- **c.5–15 AH.** **Private Bones's military period.** An Eirwyn-descended soldier is posted to a remote church outpost *during the conquest*. His remains are now in the Exchange.
- **c.10–15 AH.** **Ásdís of the Cold Eye**, an Eirwitch Warden, is burned by Mwooreth while guarding something on the eastern ridge. She is later remembered as "the witch."
- **c.12–18 AH.** Bones's church outpost becomes a fortified refuge in the concealment tradition.
- **c.20–35 AH.** **Mwooreth control peaks.** The region is conquered and the old ways survive only in hiding.
- **c.30–45 AH.** Alvar dies in his 70s or 80s. His strategy has succeeded.
- **c.50–120 AH.** Meaning fades. Church-raised generations believe the cover story *literally*.
- **c.120–170 AH.** Mwooreth fractures politically. Its cultural influence remains but its power weakens, and roads and outposts are abandoned.
- **c.170–200 AH.** The **Silent Century.** The highlands empty out.
- **c.205–215 AH.** **Varrenmoor** is founded (c.208 AH) by descendants of survivors, herders, farmers and church families.
- **312 AH.** Varrenmoor is about 104 years old, three to four generations deep.

**[UNSETTLED]** Cladis's war against Nior (Part 1.4) is **not placed on this chart**. Decide whether it falls before the Hanging or during the conquest before NPCs start quoting dates.

**What different people say about the war**
- **Villagers [COMMON]:** *"Old church stuff. Bad times. The Allfather died for us, and the dark folk had it coming."* The cover story, taken literally.
- **Pringus [ASK]:** The Exchange's ledgers record *custody*, not causes. He can tell you what was intaken and when, and nothing about who was right.
- **Mavis [EARN]:** She bought Eirwitch records and objects, so she knows pieces of Cladis's history.
- **Venfyura [EARN]:** She was *there for the truth of the Old Ways*. She knows the four lost crafts and the true nature of Mwooreth: **severance is a craft, not a sin.**
- **[SECRET]** Stellan's family has an exact role in the removed sentence. That role is **not yet defined**. Don't invent it.

## 1.4 Cladis, the Queens and the Missing Sentence
- **[COMMON in the Exchange]** There is a memorial to **Cladis**, bald and armored, with four seasonal crowns and the Winter crown uppermost.
- **[ASK]** The Eirwitches held **four ranked offices at once**:
  - **Spring:** recruitment, restoration, settlements.
  - **Summer:** roads, supplies, labor, administration.
  - **Autumn:** law, judgment, defense.
  - **Winter:** war and existential threats, with final authority, normally invoked only in crisis.
- **[ASK]** **Cladis** is the *last known Winter Queen* and led the final advance on **Nior's fortress**.
- **[EARN]** The record **stops** when Cladis enters the fortress. A precise **Mwooreth cut** removed the next sentence. It does *not* say there was a duel or who survived.
- **[SECRET]** Who cut it, why, and Stellan's family's role.
- **The Stolen Shard** (the sword) carries the inscription *"STOLEN FROM THE PLACE WHERE EVEN TIME CANNOT LEAVE."* It was taken from the **Rhime Goddess's Ice Palace** at the Wyrd's fringe, where time is frozen and the goddess is immortal. The thief, access method and permission are unknown, and no one knows whether she has noticed.

## 1.5 Varrenmoor, the Graveyards and the Seam-Wheel
- **[COMMON]** Varrenmoor is a small village facing winter hunger after the opening deaths, the fire and Father Håkon's loss. Its knocks are Hearth (2 slow, 2 fast), Kin (2 rapid), Burden (3 rapid) and Bell (4 slow).
- **[ASK]** Three graveyards lie on the road beyond Varrenmoor:
  - **Oldest** is Vaelweyr-aligned, with names like "Fornks."
  - **Middle** is Ikkander-aligned. Prickets, with some dates around 0–20 AH.
  - **Newest** is Mwooreth-aligned, beside the ruined church.
  - All three were active at the same time. The traditions were *concealed*, not replaced.
  - The chart shows the three crafts' families side by side, and the stones' names were deliberately broken away.
- **[ASK]** The **Seam-Wheel** is a carving that diagrams how the four relationships and the four crafts relate. It is a *key to the Wyrd*, **not** the entrance to the Ragged Mile.
- **[SECRET]** The **Great Tangle** is a multi-plane mystery, and the PCs hold four inherited pieces of an unfinished solution. Never tell them this directly.

## 1.6 The Four Lost Crafts
Each craft is a blend of two relationships.
- **Stellan: Runecraft** (Mwooreth + Ikkander). *Mwooreth makes the cut. Ikkander gives the order.* A carved rune can influence the Wyrd.
- **Betsy: Splicing** (Ikkander + Vaelweyr). It chooses a viable biological continuation drawn from adjacent strands.
- **Violet: Starweaving / Lifeweaving** (Vaelweyr + Eirwyn). It guides life-force through plants and fungi only.
- **Dale: Essencecraft** (Eirwyn + Mwooreth). It separates, preserves and recombines the essence a thing actually has.

**NPC knowledge:** Venfyura knows all four. The trainers know their own and only recognize the others. **Trainers teach what the PC's sheet already has and grant no extra charges.**

## 1.7 The Ossuary Exchange
- **[COMMON]** It is a **chartered neutral custody house and market** in a stable Wyrd knot with multiple thresholds. Bones are intaken, tagged, held, claimed, consigned and sold here.
- **[COMMON]** Three records must agree for any transfer: the **intake ledger**, the **custody ledger**, and the **physical tag**. Memory-bearing remains can add evidence.
- **[COMMON]** The categories are:
  - named,
  - claimed,
  - consigned,
  - unclaimed after notice,
  - construct material without identity,
  - **contracted sentient undead people**,
  - restricted material.
- **[COMMON]** **Sentient skeletal staff are people under contract, not property.** Never let them be bought.
- **[COMMON]** **Clerks are paid a fixed wage, never a commission.** Bribery is awkward, not impossible. Clerk, custodian, merchant, restorer and arbitrator are different jobs.
- **[COMMON]** **Wards** suppress deliberate violence and sabotage, **not** lies, accidents or foolishness. Outside the thresholds, the Exchange can only restrict account and access.
- **[ASK]** Mavis's estate is **beyond the eastern gate and outside Exchange jurisdiction.**
- **[ASK]** The Lower market is packed houses with bone trolleys overhead and pneumatic tubes.
- **[ASK]** **Restoration is done by independent licensed houses** under contract, with no guaranteed success. Claiming and restoring are two separate processes.
- **[ASK]** Restoration materials:
  - clean burial linen,
  - purified or distilled water,
  - a viable seed of life,
  - a protective boundary rune,
  - a joining needle and thread,
  - identity anchors,
  - a controlled place.
- **[ASK]** More skeleton gives better reconstruction data: skull or mind, then a complete spine, then pelvis, ribs and long bones, then hands and feet, then marrow. **A toe alone cannot safely reconstruct a person.**

# PART 2: THE NPCs

## LIL' APOSTROPHE (Calvin Penwick)
*"Hi. I'm Lil' Apostrophe. The apostrophe's silent, which is great, because it's the only part of me that knows when to shut up."*

**Snapshot**
- Oversized black robes, bone-tag necklaces, sideways pointed hat. Intensely pale young man with an affected "cool wizard" persona that slips the moment anyone is nice to him.
- Traces **WERD** in smoke or chalk, which makes real vocabulary magic happen. He is *great at saying it and terrible at what it means*.
- Distant relative of Mavis from another place and time. He sat unnoticed on her couch until she noticed her **inventory improved**.
- Currently working three days at the inn to cover the group's lodging.

**The recipe: Fry first, stoner second, a dash of swagger**
- **Fry core (most of the weight).** Lovably dim, endlessly sincere, loyal, always a little behind the conversation. He isn't stupid, he's *differently parsed*: he hears a sentence, picks the funnier reading and follows it all the way to its logical end.
- **Accidental wit.** His best lines are wrong on purpose-by-accident, and the logic holds up. The party laughs because it's a *better* answer than the correct one. He never knows he's being clever. If a player points it out, he's delighted and takes full credit for what he "meant."
- **Wordplay.** Puns, double meanings, mishearings and literal readings. Since his magic is *words*, language is literally his weak spot and his superpower. Bones, ledgers, tags and room are all pun fuel.
- **Stoner.** Mellow, trails off, gets distracted by something cool, then "wait, what were we doing?"
- **A dash of swagger.** One flicker of "cool guy" per scene, no more. Occasional "my guy" or "man." It cracks at once.

**The accidental-wit formula** (use this when improvising)
1. **Mishear or over-literalize** what was just said.
2. **Follow the wrong meaning through** with total sincerity.
3. **Land on something accidentally true** (or at least funnier than the right answer).
4. **Stay oblivious.** Then, a beat later, the delayed "…wait. Is that the joke?"
5. **Let the straight NPC answer.** Their dry reaction is the button.

**Speech tics**
- "Whoa." *(then silence)*
- "Wait, what was I doing?"
- "Is that the joke?"
- "I'm not a regular wizard, I'm a cool wizard." *(he wears it sideways)*
- "Shut up and take my words."
- "Okay, but *technically*…" *(he is never technically correct)*
- "That's fair." / "Cool." / "Oh."
- "I don't know what that means, but I feel it in my hat."
- *(after a pun, to himself)* "Hm. Word."

**Wants / fears**
- Wants a friend group, a sandwich and for words to *finally* do what he meant.
- Doesn't want to go back to Mavis's couch, or make anything permanent. Is scared of Mavis.

**Barks (random moments)**
- *(on arrival)* "Hey. I'm here. Is it good?"
- *(on a locked door)* "I've got a word for that. It's *knock*. Because it's a door and also, like, the sound."
- *(after a disaster)* "Okay, so that was a lot of word."
- *(told to stop)* "Yeah, no, I'm stopping." *(stops one beat late)*
- *(on seeing food)* "Is that a snack or a legal situation?"
- *(on the Stolen Shard)* "Big stick. Does it have a name, or is it still deciding?"
- *(about the Exchange)* "It's, like, a graveyard that got a business degree."
- *(about a tag)* "Tag, you're it. That's a bone joke now."
- *(about Mavis)* "She runs a tight couch."
- *(when told "that's dangerous")* "Cool. Dangerous *and* cool. Two for one."
- *(on being thanked)* "Aw. I'm going to put that in my chest and lose it by dinner."
- *(on a quiet moment)* "You ever think 'word' is just a word that's about words? Like it's showing off?"
- *(when called Calvin)* "Oh. *That* one. That's from before I was a guy."
- *(on a mistake)* "That was a mistake in the sense that it was a *make* and then a *stake*."
- *(after something goes right)* "Wow. I'm great at this. Quick, someone write it down before I stop."

**Quick Q&A (what Lil knows, and how he'd say it)**
- **Who are you?** "Lil' Apostrophe. It's possessive. I belong to the story."
- **What's Werd?** "It's words, but they listen. Sometimes too hard. W-E-R-D. Not the other one." *(He doesn't know the difference between Werd and Wyrd. If asked, he assumes they're being fancy.)*
- **What's your magic called?** "Werd. I'm a wordsmith. Like a blacksmith, but I hit things with letters. *Wait.* I'm literally a thing."
- **How does it work?** "I draw the word, the word happens. Mostly the word. Sometimes its cousins. Sometimes its cousins' *entire family*."
- **Why do you keep making ducks?** "DUCK is a very strong word, man. It's got, like, momentum. It ducks, it dives, it *flows*." *(He does not hear it.)*
- **What happened with the ducks, QUIET and LOUD?** "So I did DUCK, and there were so many ducks. Then QUIET, which was peaceful. Then LOUD, which was not. Mavis's patio was *very* loud. Mavis was also loud. It balanced. Everyone was loud, and the ducks had *great* posture."
- **Who's Mavis to you?** "Family. From, uh, another time? I sat on her couch for a while. She called me inventory. I decided that's a promotion. I'm *stock*. I'm *in* stock."
- **Is Mavis dangerous?** "Only if you're not useful. Or on her plants. Or you owe her. Or you *stand* there. Or you *sit*, which was my mistake."
- **Can you bring Bones back?** "I tried. We got Sergeant Cheese. Very melty. *Great* guy." *(sincere beat)* "He didn't deserve what happened. I mean, he was *cheese*, but he'd had a *life*." *(Stellan melted him with Burning Hands.)*
- **Why'd you join us?** "You seemed like you needed words. Also Mavis said 'go,' which was really supportive of her."
- **What's your real name?** "Calvin. But on the *record*, it's a different record."
- **What's going on in the Ossuary?** "There's bones, there's rules, and a guy who says 'no' a lot. That's the whole map. Also a *claim hold*, which I thought was a hug for a clam."
- **What's a ledger?** "A book that remembers things on purpose. Like a brain, but with fewer snacks."
- **What's the Exchange?** "You give a bone, you take a bone. It's the circle of *life*. Mostly the *death* part."
- **What's the Mwooreth thing?** "It's a word. It's got a lot of word in it. I don't draw it. It doesn't feel like a draw-it word."
- **What's the weirdest thing you've made?** "A sandwich that was also a legal contract. Don't eat the clause."
- **Do you have a plan?** "I have the *opposite* of a plan. It's, like, a vibe. Wait, what's the opposite of room?" *(Mottle: "Do not guess.")*
- **What do you know about the war?** "It was bad. Lots of, uh. Words. And sticks. Mostly sticks. And one guy who got *cut* from the book, which is rude. You don't cut a guy from the book."
- **What's it like being dead?** *(to Bones)* "Is it quiet? I mean, I tried QUIET. It was *too* quiet."

**How he reacts to each NPC (he has history with all of them)**
- **Mottle.** Filled his laundry with ducks. Mottle says "No" before Lil's finished the sentence. Lil treats it like a long friendship.
- **Pimm.** Keeps asking which one's the dog, then admits "I don't know dogs." "This place is perfect for Betsy. Everything's got bonus parts."
- **Brokka.** He has edge. He thinks that makes him a smith.
- **Quill.** Thought "Quill" was a chalk color. Calls Quill "bone wizard." Quill: "Runecrafter."
- **Pringus.** Treats him like a *very* patient librarian and asks the same question three ways.
- **Mavis.** Affectionate, scared, always asks permission, never gets it. Calls her "the boss" in a whisper.

**Plot-useful things Lil can accidentally reveal**
- **[ASK]** That Mavis's inventory is "weirdly good right now." That points at his working there.
- **[ASK]** Mavis "never uses the front door when she doesn't want to be followed."
- **[SECRET]** Any detail about *why* Mavis kept him, or his origin, is **up to you**. The handoff says only "another place and time."

**Fry-style sincerity** *(once a session, then he undercuts it himself)*
- "I don't have a lot of friends. I have, uh. Ducks. And you guys." *(beat)* "Mostly ducks."
- "I used to sit on a couch and nothing happened. Now stuff happens. It's scarier. But it's *better*."
- "I think words are how things know they're real." *(beat)* "Also how they get *fired*."

## MOTTLE, the innkeeper
*"No."*

**Snapshot.** Terse, long-suffering innkeeper. Runs a common room whose reservation slate responds to written words. Currently hosting the party under a three-day deal with Lil.

**Voice.** One-word refusals, rhetorical questions, a dry kind of tired. He *answers the question you were about to ask* and says no to it. He never raises his voice and never looks surprised.

**Wants / fears.** Wants a quiet inn and every guest accounted for. Fears Lil's laundry.

**Barks**
- "No."
- "I have not agreed to that."
- "That is not a room. That is *rumor with a bed in it.*"
- "Breakfast is included. Conditionally. On the conditions."
- "Put it back."
- "We have rules. They are posted. They are posted because of a duck."
- *(to Lil)* "Do not guess."
- *(to anyone leaving)* "Keys on the hook. All of them."

**Q&A**
- **Do you have rooms?** [COMMON] "Yes. For people who ask the right way, and a deposit."
- **Why does the slate move things?** [ASK] "It's a reservation slate. It does what is written. That is the problem."
- **What about Lil?** [COMMON] "Lil works the morning rush for three days. No ducks in the laundry. No more room-resizing. If he breaks that, he works four."
- **Prepared inn content: Room 6.** [ASK] *The Last Respite Before Further Administrative Action.* A guest has been in Room 6 for **43 years** and disputes his bill because **his body was replaced** during his stay. Mottle's line: "He claims he is a different man. I charge the same man. The ledger agrees with me."
- **Who's staying here?** [COMMON] "Bone traders, restoration clerks, a lot of people waiting on paperwork."
- **Do you know about the Exchange rules?** [ASK] "I know the ones that touch my beds."
- **Do you know about the war or Mwooreth?** [COMMON] "I know the war ended. Whoever won, they still need beds."
- **Rumors.** [ASK] Offer one per night: the pneumatic tubes carry things the clerks haven't logged, and a rat once had a legal job.

**Reward:** lodging, rumors, an introduction. **No craft feature.**

## BROKKA "THREE-FINGERS" VELL (smith and Essencecrafter)
*"Touch nothing."*

**Snapshot.** Dwarven smith with a forge full of essences and samples, short on patience. Trainer for **Dale**. She recognizes **Backwright** handling habits.

**Voice.** Gruff, short, grudging. She says what a thing *is*, not what it means. Respects work, not talk.

**Wants / fears.** Wants no one touching the workshop and a customer who knows what a clean edge is. Fears losing another finger, and the old family she half remembers.

**Barks**
- "Touch nothing."
- "That is isolated sharpness. It is not for you."
- "You have a hat on sideways."
- "Watch the edge."
- "Calvin."
- "Stop it."
- *(approving Dale)* "Hold it like that. *That.* Where'd you learn that?"
- *(about her hand)* "I had four. It was a bad bargain. Good iron."

**Q&A**
- **What's Essence of Edge?** [ASK] "A trace of isolated sharpness. Bottled. It does one thing and does it forever if you are rude."

# PART 3: LIL'S WERD MISFIRE TABLE
Roll a d12 whenever Lil traces a word. Each result is *literal, brief and fixable*.

1. **DUCK.** Ducks arrive, uncontrolled. QUIET silences them; LOUD releases it.
2. **QUIET.** Silences everything, including cancellation speech.
3. **LOUD.** Releases suppressed sound at once.
4. **DROP.** Every loose item drops.
5. **CHILL.** Freezes rather than calms.
6. **LEAVE.** Leaves, or someone's departure.
7. **FIRE.** Dismissal from employment. *Not flames.*
8. **ROOM.** The space widens, and no bedroom appears.
9. **EDGE.** Loose objects creep toward ledges. (CENTER sends them to the middle, urgently.)
10. **COPY.** Every written mark doubles.
11. **FLY.** Flies lift whatever is nearest.
12. **Pick your own** word with a *literal* result.

**Rule of thumb:** it always does what the word says, never what Lil meant. The straight NPC's reaction is half the joke.

**Opposites trap.** Lil doesn't know opposites. "What's the opposite of room?" is a question he will ask Mottle. Mottle says: "Do not guess."
