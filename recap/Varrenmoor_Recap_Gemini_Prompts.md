# Varrenmoor Recap: Gemini Prompt Pack

One prompt per recap panel (P01 to P13). The recap page has an illustration slot labeled with each ID.

## How to use this
1. Start a fresh Gemini chat per panel. Attach the character reference sheets named in the handoff (Dale the Dwarf Reference Sheet, Violet's cool-ivory sheet, Pre-/Post-Bone Soup party lineups, Pringus, Biscuit and Friends). These sheets beat any text description below.
2. Paste the **Global style block**, then the **character cards** for everyone in the panel, then the panel prompt.
3. Work one session at a time. Approve a panel before moving on, and do not regenerate approved panels after later corrections.
4. Anything marked **[CONFIRM]** was not established in the transcripts or handoff. Check it against your reference sheets before using it.

- **Stellan:** one figure only, never duplicated in a panel. Priest's son.
- **Bone Soup (Session 6) changes appear only from Session 6 on, and stay:**
  - Stellan: a tail (the later correction says tiefling-style, not kangaroo), feline paws, altered throat, cold breath.
  - Betsy: blue nose, gills, a missing finger, a compressed spine.

- **Horses:** Harper and Thunder are different horses. Do not merge them.
- **Ladders:** a ladder tips backward, away from the cliff or ledge, so the fall reads coherently.
- **Mwooreth** is an ordinary name in-world. Never joke about its spelling in art.

## Panel prompts

### P01. Session 1: Embers of Varrenmoor
**Primary.** Eastern ridge at dusk. Dale stands on top of a wood-and-lashing ladder about 40 ft up a cliff face, a pot still bubbling beside him. The ladder is tipping BACKWARD away from the cliff. A rope tied to a stout tree is in his hand as he starts to rappel. Birds wheel overhead, campfire smoke below. Small in the foreground, Violet runs after Kenko. Looking-up camera to sell the height.
**Alt.** Thorn farm at night: a smoke-filled door with a beam jammed at about 45 degrees, the barn burning beside it, Dale bashing the barn door as a draft horse bolts out. Orange firelight, black smoke.
**Alt 2.** Church, about 9 p.m.: Father Håkon laid out on a pew, Dale holding up his legs, Violet praying beside him, Kenko licking his face. Torch sconces, long shadows.
**Don't:** show Betsy rescuing the horse (Dale does).

### P02. Sessions 2–3: The Night of the Nox
Inside the barred church at night: sleeping children on pews, bodies under one blanket, torch sconces, no windows, a heavy bolt and latch. Violet holds the door. A dented door in the back with Kenko growling at it. In the dark yard, a shadow-shaped Nox dissolves under Dale's club while the horse Harper stomps. Faint warmth returns, crickets begin again.
**Characters:** Dale, Violet, Stellan (with a poker, casting Light, a 10-ft dim glow), Betsy (with a shovel), Kenko, Harper.
**Don't:** show a sustained siege. It is one night.

### P04. Session 4: The Pilgrim Shelter
A rough pilgrim shelter in the hills: dark blood on the floor, Håkon's torn cloak and a note pinned there. Outside, a lowered drawbridge, a key tucked into tree roots, a raven carrying a key from an old church ruin. The party silhouetted in grey hill light.
**Note:** recap-only session. Keep it moody and small.

### P05. Session 5: Four Keys and an Egg
**Primary.** Violet kneels beside an open chest. Her hand is on Kenko's back and her amulet is in her other hand, the egg (a green-and-purple crystal-like egg, about 20 lb) before her. Light runs from the egg to the amulet and the fox. The surroundings fade into a calm, clear, starry meadow at night with crickets, visible only around her.
**Alt.** A split tree trunk with a crack down the middle: Biscuit climbs with a rope, a branch shudders, Dale hauls.
**Alt 2.** Bones sits up in a shallow grave of loose fresh dirt, finger bones poking out, picks up a stick like a spear and salutes.
**Don't:** make the egg glow warm or put it in a marble chest.

### P06. Session 6: Leave a Bone, Take a Bone
Fog-shrouded clearing. A hooded vendor with an empty hood holds a ladle over a bubbling cauldron. Bones lies in a heap of bones at its edge. A tattered beggar cuts a rib out and drinks three drops: wings erupt and his boots split into talons. The party looks on, Violet clearly refusing.
**After this panel:** apply the Bone Soup changes from the continuity rules.

### P07. Session 7: Market of Transients
**Primary.** Venfyura's tent: overlapping patchwork cloth, a desert glimpsed inside larger than the tent. Mismatched eyes, threads running from her rings to bells and teeth. A linen-wrapped bone frozen in a bowl of water; a white thread pulled up, a glowing Bones taking shape. Violet's amulet glows.
**Alt.** Kefek Vegge on a stool, tracing a cardboard-flap pop-up book with a giant finger, a wagon of jars, feathers, cracked glass and small skulls behind him.

### P08. Session 8: Talskc's Bargain
Talskc behind his stall, cleaning a jar and catching gold that drips from his raised hand, eyeing the egg poking out of Violet's pack. A fish stand nearby. In his other hand a slender, unnaturally gray, serrated bone with black tooth-like spikes and a barcode-like tag. Behind the party, a wagon of cheese wheels, salted eel and packed meats.

### P09. Session 9: The Narrowing Road
A winter forest road at dusk, a blizzard camp with a small fire. Violet holds up her glowing amulet; a snake-shaped constellation has stepped out of the sky and winds along the path like a glowing guide. A skeleton shape lurks at the edge of the firelight.
**Note:** recap-only session.

### P10. Session 10: Three Graveyards
**Primary.** A full-moon basin where snow stops at the forest's edge like a curtain. Three graveyards (oldest left, middle straight ahead, newest right beside a roofless ruined church), a slowly orbiting floating rune overhead. Stellan, 5 to 10 ft from it, copies runes with pen and paper. A 20-ft briar wall has grown behind the party.
**Alt.** The cave: torchlight on dark cold water, frost-rimed empty bedrolls with body-shaped dents, a hissing badger cornered. A rope bridge with Dale slipping.

### P11. Session 11: The Blob in the Cave
Biscuit peeks over a ledge: a convulsing mass of rotting flesh with faces and arms about 40 ft up, dripping orange goo onto Dale below. Violet on a wet cliff catches Dale mid-fall above icy water, eyes locked. Torch-only lighting, fog.
**Alt.** A fake Dale beside a bonfire with blue flames, real Dale's spectral dwarf ancestors around him, souls streaming out of cracks like bats.

### P12. Session 12: The Sword and the Clerk
**Primary.** A statue hall in blue lantern light. Two party members lift a heavy ice-blue greatsword (about 6 ft) by its ends after it clangs to the floor. A wall slot waits for it.
**Alt.** Pringus at his clerk's workstation: a skeleton in spectacles stamping papers, a glass tube with brass stoppers sliding into an overhead pneumatic tube, bone carts above, a floor square labelled "femoral intake".
**Alt 2.** The smoke room: skunk-sweet smoke layered at the ceiling, hanging plants turning toward the party, ducks dropping from Lil's sleeve and ceiling as he writes DUCK with a long pipe.

### P13. Session 13: Mottle's Mugs
**Primary.** Mavis's hallway: Lil holds a bone to his ear, a smoke cloud around him, scribbled air-writing, and a yellow, stinky cheese skeleton giving a thumbs-up. Golden starry word-essence glows at the bases of plants.
**Alt.** Lower market stone wall: Biscuit hangs about 16 ft up from a rope, crates and carts toppled, counterweights clunking in the wall, Violet pulling.
**Alt 2.** Mottle's Mugs common room, long and stretching about 30 ft, warm lamplight, an innkeeper at the counter, the party at a long table at about 8 p.m.
