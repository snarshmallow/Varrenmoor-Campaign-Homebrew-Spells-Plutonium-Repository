# How to Join Our D&D Game Online

*(Drafted with ChatGPT 2026-11-08 from facts supplied by the GM; light edits only. Review before sending to players.)*

Welcome to Varrenmoor! Joining our online D&D game is easy, and there's nothing to download or install. Just follow these steps.

## 1. Open the game website
Go to **https://foundry.varrenmoor.com**
- Use a normal web browser. Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a computer works best. Tablets are okay too.

## 2. Sign in (choose one option)
The first screen is a Cloudflare sign-in page. You have two ways in:

**Option A: Discord**
1. Click **Discord**.
2. Sign in with the same Discord account you use for our server.
3. You need the **Varrenmoor Campaign** role. If it doesn't work, ask the GM.

**Option B: One-time PIN (email)**
1. Choose **One-time PIN**.
2. Enter the email address you gave the GM.
3. Check your email for a 6-digit code from Cloudflare.
4. Type that code in to continue.

## 3. Join your character
1. On the Foundry join screen, pick your character's user name from the dropdown.
2. Enter the password the GM gave you.
3. Click **Join Game**.

## 4. Next time you play
Your sign-in lasts 24 hours. After that, sign in again with Discord or an email code.

## Having trouble?
- **"Access denied"**: tell the GM so they can add you.
- **Blank page**: press **Ctrl + F5** to reload everything.
- **Maps or audio lagging**: close other browser tabs and use a strong or wired internet connection.

## One last thing
Please keep the game link and sign-in codes within our group. Don't share them with anyone outside the campaign.

Ask the GM in Discord if anything looks wrong.
